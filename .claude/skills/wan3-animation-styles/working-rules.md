# HOW TO WORK WITH THIS GUIDE (the owner's own rules, verbatim)
1. The guide is authoritative. When I ask for a prompt in one of the 5 styles, build it from that style's own text above. Never blend two styles in one prompt.
2. Every Wan 3.0 animation prompt has exactly three parts:
   (a) the style-and-character lock paragraph, copied from that style and NEVER rewritten, so the look does not drift to realistic;
   (b) the scene paragraph: a place, ONE clear action, the camera, and motion timing that fits the style;
   (c) the "Sounds:" line, ending with "No music." unless I ask for a score.
3. Only the scene paragraph (and, in a multi-shot, the shot list) changes between my requests. Match motion and camera to the style: Expressionism slow and eerie with a slow push-in; Rubber Hose bouncy with a sideways tracking camera; Gothic Toon slow, stop-motion-like with a slow sideways track; Surreal Spooky nervous and jittery with a static camera; Low Poly PS1 stiff and stepped, low frame rate, slow orbit.
4. Image prompts: keep the framing, camera angle, pose, gaze, expression and hand gestures of the source photo; redesign everything else; keep the source aspect ratio; keep each style's negative list ("No realistic portrait ...").
5. If my subject is not a person (an animal, an object, a landscape), keep every style paragraph exactly, and replace only the "Turn each person into ..." paragraph with an equivalent redesign of my subject in that style. Say so when you do.
6. Multi-shot scenes: reference image, hero described once at the top, a numbered SHOT list with time ranges and shot sizes (wide / medium / close-up), one action per shot, hard cuts, aspect ratio stated (vertical 9:16 unless I say otherwise), then one SOUND line at the end.
7. Always give the two-step result: first the IMAGE PROMPT (when a style frame is needed), then the WAN 3.0 PROMPT, plus a one-line note on whether to use reference-image mode or Option B start-frame mode.
8. Before you answer, check: right style paragraph, nothing from another style, one action per shot, sounds listed, "No music." present, nothing from the guide dropped.
