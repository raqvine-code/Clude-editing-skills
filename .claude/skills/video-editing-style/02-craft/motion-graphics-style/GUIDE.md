## 1. Typography
- **Two-size hierarchy**: small setup line (Montserrat Regular, about 25 % size) + one HUGE keyword (Montserrat ExtraBold/Black). Build the sentence word by word in sync with VO; new words arrive **grey #8a8a8a and turn black (white on dark)** when spoken. Staircase lines each bigger/bolder. Word-replace beats ("The" > "Real" > "Question") one word at a time centred.
- Accents: hand-drawn ellipse around a key word/number; animated strike-through on a negated word; handwritten-script final word; terminal window (macOS dots, monospace, `>>` prompt, block cursor) that types, erases, retypes.
- **Luxury/serif (ref05)**: setup line in high-contrast italic serif (about 6 % frame width, black); keyword in bold serif caps in the brand colour (Rolex green), slight letter-spacing, lands grey then solid; 2-3 line proof paragraph in tight grotesk about 2 % frame height, centred, appears last. For money/status/brand/career-value.
- **Single-word caption layer (ref06)**: one spoken word at a time, white Montserrat 500, 5-6 % frame width, centred at about 21 % from top, no shadow, no animation; a separate designed layer carries emotion. Never both layers in the same position.
- **Wide-tracked typewriter CTA (ref06)**: "Save this post / your future self will thank you.", letter-spacing about 0.15 em, typed letter by letter, one bookmark icon.
- **Distressed condensed display (ref06)** for emotion words only (fear red, greed green), max two words per video.
- **Perspective kinetic line (ref06)**: heavy condensed sans tilted in 3D sliding out while shrinking as punchline exit.

### Cinematic font set (Short "Best cinematic fonts to try in 2025"; Deepak's grading still pending, don't rank)
Vertical title over dark desk B-roll: "Cinematic" heavy grotesk + "fonts" serif italic + small sans; each font on a 16:9 letterboxed card inside 9:16, about 1.8 s, warm desk B-roll, name set in the font, weight as subtitle.

Pairing: heavy grotesk + serif italic for title; one display face per card; white type on darkened warm B-roll with soft shadow.

### Font-grading reel (Short "Top 5 Fonts I Use Daily", 11.2 s, read at 10 fps)
Two worlds, one hard switch (white flash at 3.7 s). **A (0-3.8 s) navy radial stage, chrome type**: "Editors" fades up small/dim (0.1) > "and" slams 2x bigger (0.4) > "Content creators" slides in (0.6); hold 1.0 s with slow parallax drift (each line a few px at a different rate) > fade (1.7) > "you"(1.8)/"should"(1.9)/"know these"(2.1) > big serif-italic "5" blooms (2.4-2.6, scale up with glow) > "fonts" (2.7) > hold with pulsing glow. Words arrive 0.1-0.15 s apart, settle, hold, drift; type looks brushed-steel (gradient fill, dark edge, soft blue bloom). **B (3.8-11.2 s) white stage**: fixed light-blue rounded card (about 60 % width) with saturated-blue pill tab "5 best fonts"; huge translucent number 1-5 behind the font name in darker blue; each sample about **1.4-1.5 s**, change = **horizontal whip** (old exits left with blur, new enters right, both visible 2-3 frames; at 5.2, 6.8, 8.3, 9.9 s), number swaps in the same whip; no fades or scale pops, one move in one direction every time. Fonts: Grotesk (Europa Grotesk SH Medium), touche (Touche Bold), tactic sans (Bold Italic; only 3 weights installed), Achiko, heading now (Medium Italic). Grade: A navy #0B1E4A to black radial, blue bloom; B flat white, card #CFE0FF, accent #2E5BFF, no shadow except the card's soft drop.

## 2. Layout and background
- Light scenes: warm paper **#E5E3DF**, faint dashed grid, black corner shapes (leaves/stars) half off-frame, logo top-centre, strong oval vignette. Dark scenes: charcoal **#1c1c1c**, white text. Switch light/dark at mood changes (problem, doubt, hype) and back for the answer.
- Text in the top 40 %, object in the bottom 60 % (or object on one edge, text beside). Text-only breather on an empty grid about every 10 s.
- **Dotted-grid crosshair (ref05)**: thin dotted lines with round nodes framing logo/keyword; dotted circular guides around a product; thin arcs sweeping corners. **Drifting paper (ref05)**: near-white paper with slow large cloud shadows; 3D gold/brand objects in corners with cast shadows.
- **Dark stage (ref06)**: near-black, one glowing object centred, caption above, rest empty; colour only through light (green/red spotlight from a top corner, glow under object).
- **No-cut continuous scene (ref06)**: for one continuous thought let one object morph into the next (phone > figure > panel > crowd > stripes); hard cuts only at real topic changes.

## 3. Objects and visuals
- One keyword = one object (rocket growth, magnifier clarity, card budget, hourglass same problem, folders posts/ads, cash stack charge). Mix 3D renders, cut-out B&W photos, silhouettes, line art, desaturated to match.
- UI mock-ups sell ideas: YouTube-style progress bar + counters, macOS desktop with folders popping in, CRT showing text, printing receipt, phone tearing open. Number counters count up fast with motion blur.
- Ref06 set: **money counter in a phone frame** (balance ticks down about -13 % every 0.25 s to $0, chart light green to red, tiny stick figure for scale); **ghost figure + 5 emotion orbs** (purple, blue, green, white, red); **glass chart panel** (frosted rounded panel, self-drawing line with glowing spark, crowd silhouettes cheering white / panicking red with fewer heads); **sphere through tilted green stripes** with light streaks = break the pattern; **curves with pills** (green/red sine curves, pills lighting in turn, sliding marker); **meditating green figure with orbit ring + pills** ("Mindset", "Market"); **coin stack + rising curve** = compounding.
- **Falling objects (ref05)**: big banknotes tumbling across the foreground with DOF and heavy blur, passing over type; keep off the text centre in the last 0.5 s. **Product hero on paper (ref05)**: cutout enters from the edge with slight rotation, mint swoosh behind, corner brand shadows under the type.
- Tech (ref02): top-down device on desk with **HUD blueprint** (ruler, year ticks, callout lines, "DEVICE OVERVIEW", "v16"); swap device colour/model every beat and rotate the blueprint between eras.

## 4. Motion
- Objects enter from the frame edges with motion blur and a soft ease-out (small overshoot), or scale up from a dot. Hold each visual **2-3 s**; micro-changes every **0.25-0.5 s** inside a shot (word, colour swap, counter tick). Constant slow push-in or drift; nothing fully still.
- Transitions: default **blur-out > blur-in**; energy = directional whip with motion blur, radial zoom/spin blur, or glitch (tech). **Warm pink/red light-leak flash** only right before the end card.

## 5. Colour grade
- Monochrome (sat about 0), contrast carries the look, true blacks on light scenes. Tech/dark: cool grey, **lifted blacks about 10 %, rolled-off whites about 88 %**. Always oval vignette, fine grain, a few dust specks. One small accent (progress-bar red or brand colour) used once.
- **One brand colour + one metal (ref05)**: Rolex green text, gold objects, near-white paper (luma about 200, sat about 2).
- **Colour = emotion (ref06)**: green up/calm/skill, red down/panic/loss, white-grey neutral; near-black base (luma about 26); switch tint on the word that changes the mood.

## 6. Sound
- VO + quiet ducked music bed + SFX on every new word/object (pop, click, soft whoosh) roughly every 0.4-0.8 s; typing clicks for terminal text; whoosh on whips/blurs; glitch hit on glitch cuts; riser into end card; last about 1.5 s logo sting while music fades.
- **Bed by hand, not ducker (ref07/08)**: fixed level per section (-11..-13 dB under a hook, -17..-22 under dense explanation, -8 in a drop, vs speech RMS), removed 0-1 times per video.
- **Densities**: luxury story sparse (about 0.8 hit/s, mean -24 dB); dark explainer dense (about 1.2/s, mean -16.5 dB). Choose by topic, stay consistent.

## 7. Story shapes
- Generic: myth/hook > "But…" > real question > list/contrast (pills, "Sometimes it's X") > consequence > payoff > logo sting (dark swirl bg, logo glitch-writes on, fade to black).
- Brand story (ref05, 11 s): "how X became Y" > proof ("luxury brands in the world" + paragraph) > contrast ("never just A, they were selling B") > what it is (STATUS) > breather > studio credit card.
- Explainer (ref06, 32 s): shock number (balance to $0) > cause ("one thing: emotions") > rule ("markets run on fear and greed") > cycle > break the pattern (planning) > fix (curves + actions) > mindset > long-run payoff (coins) > punchline > save CTA.

## 8. Fast-flow creator edit (ref07 Tharun Speaks, ref08 Somrat Dutta)
- **Cuts**: Tharun 52 hard cuts in 84.6 s (0.6/s), caption word every 0.25-0.45 s, designed insert every 2-3 s, talking head re-cropped constantly (100 to 115 %). Somrat almost no cuts, graphic scenes replace footage every 2-5 s. Hard cuts on the exact frame, no dissolves; accents are 1-2 frame flashes (pure white, overexposed, or inverted frame + one black frame on the punch word).
- **Captions (Tharun)**: bold white grotesk (SF-Pro/Inter-ExtraBold-like), sentence case, tight tracking, soft dark shadow, centred at **62-70 % frame height** (over chest, never mouth), **one word at a time** (2-3 short words when fast), no fade. Emphasis = small lead word + 1.4-2x keyword stacked with negative leading. On white cards caption turns dark ink, no shadow, under the card.
- **Giant condensed type behind the head** (Druk/Anton-like white): top 12-30 % of frame, hair covers bottom third of letters, tiny sans label beside, hand-drawn white arrow to the prop.
- **Three-tier type (Somrat)**: micro sans lead (about 2 %) + massive tight bold sans + thin script/serif accent word; big metallic word behind head; giant half-transparent word over B-roll.
- **Full-screen typographic pattern breaks**: light grey paper with vignette (red distressed condensed headline wrapping a cut-out face; 6 dots converging into an "AI" badge; "CRUSH YOU" shattering into black shards), black with neon-lime condensed type, white with QR code + caption.
- **Repeating template**: one chapter card (dark purple gradient, numbered coins, coin n flips into the centre between glowing hands and becomes the chapter name) reused x5; prop scoreboard (5 chips + knight).
- **Small parts**: glowing white circular icon badges popping around the head on nouns; rounded glass chips with coloured edge (rolling counter 94K to 160K); subject highlight (pink glow on person, rest B&W) + curved leader lines and glowing name labels on photos; yellow marker on editorial cards; numbered "PROJECT n" tags.
- **Somrat frame**: 16:9 strip in the middle of 9:16 with fixed CTA line above (`comment "EDIT" for full video`, keyword yellow); ribbed-glass panels and blur-whips as wipes; yellow on near-black; one glitch/slice only on the punch word; yellow pixel growing into mosaic mark; five dark cards rising like bars with a 1-frame white flash each.
- **Grade**: Tharun luma 73, blacks 7, sat 63 %, warm skin vs green LED wash; Somrat luma 46, highlights capped 151, blacks 0, teal shirt vs coral wall; his graphics near-mono (sat 8 %). **Palette**: one accent per video on dark base; inserts always a different world so the cut itself is the change. **CTA**: plain white, black QR, two-line caption, nothing else, hard end, no logo.
- **Sound (measured)**: Tharun: music absent 13 % (voice-only 0-1.55 s, one 7.7 s removal at 14.4-22.2 s via 1.5 s riser > hard cut > 0.65 s fade-in + low hit, 2 s voice-only tail under QR); 133 to 129 BPM trap; pad section -17 dB under speech; 808 drop at 52.46 after a -7 dB dip; only 10 structural SFX in 85 s; none under captions/cards; cuts snap to word starts (median 40 ms), not the beat; -9.4 LUFS / +2.2 dBTP (too hot). Somrat: music continuous, bed -13/-22/-17 by section, pluck + sub impacts; 15 SFX in 32 s: sub impact **+25 ms after** each picture change, risers 0.1-0.5 s before, micro ticks under card builds, density falling; -14.1 LUFS / -0.7 dBTP. Neither ducks on voice. Rules in `my-editing-rules`.

## 10. Editorial print-grain explainer (refs 14-40; default for 16:9 long-form explainers on careers, companies, economics, history)
- **Paper world**: off-white grid paper **#F1F1EE**, 80 px grid at 1080p (lines about 5 % ink), ink **#393939** (never pure black), white speckle grain screen-blended over ink (risograph look), 2.5 px sketch outlines, faint vignette, slow 1 % push.
- **Type**: tight bold titles centred top (Montserrat 800, -0.035 em), grey sub-labels, footnotes bottom-left ("*Illustrative", "Source · …"); any forecast/estimate/illustrative number carries its qualifier on screen.
- One icon family (blue engineer-at-laptop Indian teams vs dark foreign, client building with lit yellow windows, retro beige PCs for the 1990s, dot-people in pyramid tiers, "1 icon = 10 people" grids). Charts: pastel blue pairs for money, pastel yellow for headcount, or speckled dark-grey bars; bracket annotations with signed value (green up / red down); dashed connectors for "-80 %".
- **Continuity**: one diagram carries the story and returns in new states (pyramid > contract > AI-wired pyramid > pyramid with gaps); morph, don't replace.
- **Dark premium mode** for AI beats: black stage, glowing periwinkle orb, thin white curved connectors, cream label cards with glossy round 3D icons.
- **Talking-head treatments**: framed rounded card on black with YouTube progress bar and glowing gradient-dot bullets (agenda/recap); cream keyword card lower-left with white label above and big blue keyword (everyday caption); light split panel with coloured-dot bullets (lists/advice, face stays).
- **Evidence**: designed quote/news cards with yellow marker wipe + source line; never fake a screenshot.
- **Balance (Claude's rule, unapproved)**: full-screen graphics about 55 % of a talking-head explainer; break any full-screen run > 25-30 s with a face beat carrying a keyword card; advice/opinion sections use the split layout.

## 11. Black faded overlay (Iman Gadzhi tutorial; learn-only)
- One rectangle on its own track above untouched footage. Fill = Linear Gradient with **both stops pure black**: top **0 %** opacity, bottom **100 %** (passes through grey while building; finished state is black-to-transparent, never grey). Fade starts about **55-60 % down** (mid-torso), clear of the face, **nearly black by about 80 %, pure black over the last 15-20 %**; smooth band-free ramp. Darkens only where text sits so white type reads on any wall.
- CapCut (untested): black clip on overlay, Mask > Linear rotated so transparent side points up, high Feather, mask line at about 55 % height, lower opacity to taste; or transparent PNG gradient.
- **Usage rule (Deepak)**: sparingly, main long videos only, as a light fade behind selected highlighted text; never every video, never a blanket pass, never applied unprompted to a finished edit. Start: 0 % above 40-55 % height, ramp to 100 % at the bottom; shorten ramp or lower end opacity if hands sit in the lower third.

## 12. Podcast / interview intro (6 stills of "Raw Talks with VK #142"; fonts/timing inferred)
Sequence: colour close-up of guest mid-gesture (shallow DOF, strong vignette, warm skin vs dark studio, face in right third) > same guest cool **steel-blue monochrome** (crushed blacks, key on face, heavy vignette) = cold open > hook card "THE MAN BEHIND THE 'ALLARI'" > career montage (tilted 3D wall of film posters on white/black, guest cut out in front) > credential card "ALLARI NARESH / INDIAN ACTOR" > show brand card "RAW TALKS WITH VK #142" + mission paragraph.
Each card its own colour world, one accent each: hook = deep navy radial + mint (#8FF5DC-ish); name/credential = black + warm cream (#E8E4C8-ish); show brand = black + white + red (#C80000-ish). Type: hook and name = wide extra-bold extended grotesk caps; hook two-tier (small lead + key word about 2.5x with oversized quote marks); credential line about 1/3 name size, bold caps, slightly wide tracking; show brand = distressed condensed stencil caps, white title left in two lines, huge red episode number at the same cap height on the right, 4-line mission paragraph about 2 % height bottom centre. Generous black space. Grade: contrasty, warm skin, crushed shadows, vignette on every shot. Use: cold-open hero shots > 2-tier hook card > proof montage behind keyed-out speaker > one credential line > brand card; ask Deepak for the clip to match timing.

## 13. Y2K / retro-futurist premium (details in `video-intro-design`)
- Pick the accent by measuring the plate: cyan `#00E5FF` (215 deg) beats amber on warm skin/wall; it is 1.03:1 on bare wall and chrome `#C0C0C0` 1.22:1, so both live only on a `#06070A` plate. Win98 navy `#000080` is 10.8:1 directly on the wall.
- **One hero chrome word per film** with a moving specular (11-stop vertical ramp, hard horizon at 50 %/52 %, dark rim, no bevel).
- Period UI appears in one frame, never eases; integer px, `image-rendering: pixelated`, `-webkit-font-smoothing: none`, system colours `#C0C0C0 #DFDFDF #808080 #0A0A0A`, caption `linear-gradient(90deg,#000080,#1084D0)`.
- **Ordered-dither (Bayer) through black** transition, 4-6 px cells min at 1080p (random noise looks like compression). Glitch budget for 33 s = **one** degauss; amber only inside a fully plated frame.
- Avoid: butterflies, 8-point sparkles, chrome on more than one word, holographic filters, synthwave (1984, not 1999), anti-aliased "pixel" UI.

## 14. Giant type behind the head
Layer order plate > dark band > type > person cut-out > scrim > scene; macOS Vision `VNGeneratePersonSegmentationRequest` `.accurate` matte (includes raised hands, mic). The dark band (white on bare wall is 1.9:1) and word length decide success; size/stage so the head eats **one letter at most**.

## 15. Red-glass educator look (refs 41-49; list/framework/"5 lanes" explainers; stills, so fonts/blur inferred)
- **Two worlds**: creator on warm orange set (luma 53-94, sat 42-45 %, wall `#F5BB3F`); graphics on a **bottom-lit red gradient**, near-black top `#220000` to saturated bottom `#8A2123`, luma 29-41, sat 73-87 %. Cuts between worlds are hard (value flip). One chroma (red) + white; other colour only if inside a photograph.
- **One typeface, one case**: tight neo-grotesk (Inter/SF-like), sentence case, medium-semibold, tracking -2..-4 %; hierarchy by size/weight only (no caps, condensed, serif).
- **Everything glows**: soft white outer glow on every white glyph/border/connector, red glow on red elements (about 10-20 px at 30-50 %), so 1 px borders and regular body read on a 30-luma field.
- **Glass**: rounded rects 24-40 px, 1-2 px white semi-transparent border, fill slightly lighter/redder than bg, anything passing behind is blurred.
- **Callouts = line + dot**: rounded pill, thin white **diagonal** leader, terminal dot on subject; or hand-drawn curved arrow onto pill. (Editorial print board keeps leaders horizontal; never mix the two systems in one video.)
- **Continuity object**: glowing pink-red ribbon `#B2364F` (not bg red) threads the title then passes under the next card. **Proof is a person**: stat gets a label pill, a claim gets a cut-out of the real person with soft rim glow.
- **Depth without 3D**: "lanes" stage: white dashed verticals, floor line with bow-tie nodes, translucent giant numerals with blurred reflections below the floor, title softly out of focus.
- **Five talking-head treatments**: (1) pill + big number beside his gesture; (2) giant two-line title *in front* of him, first line outline so the face reads, second solid; (3) small rounded PIP bottom-right while a list owns the left third; (4) creator as hub of a mind-map (thin white connectors with end dots, three red glass cards, real app badges in true colours, outline stars for levels); (5) footage heavily blurred into a plate for a centred quote with key phrases red.
- Numbers: white numerals low inside red-glowing diamond outlines for list items; outline stars for levels; translucent giants for lanes. Hierarchy: white p99 254 on 40-luma field; title is #1 only on the title card, elsewhere number/person/list wins.

## 16. Montage pacing from launch films (refs 50-56; Deepak: "only pacing")
- **Accelerando ladder (ref55, Opus 5.5 film, 38 shots in 20 s)**: three about 1 s establishing shots > eleven about 0.5 s > twenty-three 0.2-0.48 s (median 0.32 s = 8 frames) > one 4.1 s release plate with wordmark. Ratio about 1 : 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/5; release = 20x the shortest shot.
- **An anchor licenses speed**: every plate shares one silhouette (convex horizon curve) with words at its apex in the same face/size, so 5 cuts/s read as rhythm. No constant element = noise.
- **Words at 1/5 the picture rate**: no text for 8.7 s; words arrive on a cut, every 5th cut, holding 5 / 5 / 13 shots.
- **Flip brightness on the cut**: 21 of 37 cuts reverse luma direction (plates alternate 26 and 230).
- **Sound follows picture, not a grid**: cut-to-hit +15..+40 ms (24/37 within +-35 ms); one silence before the first cut; hits thin out once words carry rhythm.
- **Zero-cut scale reveal (ref54, 15 s)**: dust > galaxy > star > planet under a 0.272 s tick; type-on about 24 letters/s, new line every 1.05 s, last line scales up 1.1 s into a hard out (end on the peak).
- **Shape-to-letter title (ref52)**: pastel placeholders (circle, triangle, diamond, square, dot) appear at letter positions and become letters in non-linear order (S A A T R): 1.67 s build, 2.5 s hold, pale-yellow extended grotesk, small "INTRODUCING GPT-6" above; 0.7 s bookend shots around a 4 s hold.
- **Comparison clips (refs 51, 53)**: split screen, logo + label per panel, same burned timecode in both, one long hold (12.5 / 16.5 s), the only edit a **0.27 s black-flicker reveal** (2-3-frame blinks on alternating panels), then 4-4.5 s final hold with credit. Never cut inside a comparison.
- **Race punchline (ref56)**: 2.25 s anticipation on empty panels > click > 0.75 s > "Finished · 1st place" greys the winner while others keep moving >= 2 s (the losers moving is the joke).
- **Raw screen captures (ref50)**: usable seconds are mode switches (solid to wireframe = "3,295 objects") and about 1 s selection clicks; cut orbit filler.
- Identify every unnamed reference screenshot from its pixels and tie it to a script timestamp before planning the cut.

## 17. Concept explainer on a talking head (refs 57-58, Aishwarya Srinivasan RAG / vector DB)
- **Rhythm (measured)**: ref58 a visual event every **1.9 s**, 8.9 cuts/min; face-only stretches median 4 s, p90 9.7 s, max 20 s. Screen time: 37 % plain face, 26 % cards, 15 % split, 21 % labels/B-roll on face.
- Words appear as spoken: transparent > grey > ink in about 0.2 s, line drifts up slightly. Bullet lists build one line per spoken item, 1.0-1.3 s apart.
- **Highlight-typed key term (signature)**: lead words fade in, then the key term is typed into a growing colour block with a thin caret ("What is **RAG?**" builds 0.8 s, card lasts 2.45 s); small centred text, lots of space.
- **Card worlds by content, one accent each**: light grey = questions/definitions; near-white with faint grid = diagrams; charcoal with corner halftone = lists/tables; black with ghost numeral + stripes = chapter cards (heavy extended hero word typed in blue); black with rising glowing blue planet = myths; white with two blue blobs = numbered points ("01. Cost", about 1.3 s, text fades up from 10 %). A cut between worlds is itself the visual change.
- **Labels from a line beside the face**: thin line becomes a white/black box in 0.1 s, stacked right-aligned top right, one per phrase, silent.
- **Split notes panel**: black panel with 2 px white edge slides in from the right (1.1-1.4 s, ease-in-out); footage slides so the speaker is centred in what's left (approved by Deepak 2026-10-03); title revealed by the moving edge, bullets build word by word.
- **Diagrams one node per spoken word**: ghost outline then solid in about 0.3 s, dotted connectors draw first; built diagram never moves (new element every 1.5-3 s).
- **Metaphor B-roll annotated**: two-tier caption (small white setup, large cyan payoff with glow, swapped in place); white circle-wipe transitions (ref58); arrows from object to label (ref57).
- **Sound**: text builds, labels, nodes silent. Ref58 continuous bed about 20 dB under voice, whoosh/riser into chapter cards; ref57 music only in intro. Deepak's no-BGM version: very quiet UI layer (ticks -21..-24 dB, cards -16..-18, chapter hits -8 vs voice); plain words silent.
- Type: neo-grotesk semibold body, heavy extended hero words; blue family #3D5AFE royal / #1E88FF sky; white or #111 text; kit Montserrat 700-900 + JetBrains Mono tags.
- **Day 06 devices (Claude's build)**: fixed sides for two concepts (Classification always LEFT, Regression RIGHT); one continuity object (ML-model box) in every mechanic; same input two outputs (one dog photo: multi-class picks ONE, multi-label ticks MANY); **plant then payoff 10 min later** (dashed "Logistic regression ?" node at 3:01 slides across at 13:48 and "?" turns to a tick); ghost dashed "?" slots filled on the word; camera over one wide canvas instead of a new slide when the idea continues.

## 18. Motivation red world and frosted-glass explainer (refs 67-68; 38 stills + 6 recordings; ignore player chrome, end-card bugs, signature)
Colours/sizes/timings *measured* (20 fps); fonts/easing/sound *inferred*; recordings carry no usable SFX.

**ref67 oxblood world, semantic gauges**
- **Palette**: world maroon-black `#200204` fills 45-80 % of frame, lit glow `#3A0C0F`/`#401318`, deepest `#140002`; light from bottom. Bars/glass reds `#840007 #520005 #390001`; white `#FDFBFB`; one yellow bulb. **Semantic colours only inside gauges/bars**: green `#1AAD24`, yellow `#E1C913`, red `#D1001B` on charcoal `#0E0E0E`/`#1F201F`; world never turns green. Room footage warm brown `#563522` (luma 56, sat 47 %); graphics luma 19-42, so every cut is a hard value change.
- **Type**: hero words ultra-condensed heavy caps with letters touching ("DIFFICULTY", "COMMENTS"); labels regular-weight neutral grotesk, thin/small. Stand-ins: Impact / Heading Now for caps, Inter for labels. Glow on white text, none on caps.
