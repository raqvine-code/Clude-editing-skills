# Style profiles (pick ONE)

## Tharun Speaks 9:16 (default for Deepak's talking heads; measured)
- **Voice leads.** Captions: one line at y 55-68 % of height, white Inter Tight Bold 116 px, hard pops with a median beat of 6 f at 25 fps.
- **Stacks:** small lead + big keyword + small trailer.
- **Giant caps behind the head** (Bebas/Anton), slightly blurred and glowing.
- **Pictures:** every noun gets one (badge, card, logo, number) on its word.
- **Jump cuts and pushes:** jump cuts at 127-143 %; hook push 100 → 116 % over 43 f; slow push 1.2-3.6 %/s.
- **Cutaways:** graphic scenes of 1.1-3.4 s, about a third of the runtime.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts, plus negative flash, white flash and blur-zoom.
- **Colour:** one accent family (lime on black, or navy + neon green).
- **Structure:** cut about every 2 s. Open hard; end on the ask.
- **Sound:** a loud, voice-forward master; low hits on reveals.

Full detail: `tharun-speaks-edit-style` (and its references) for exact tokens and scene recipes.

## Hormozi talking head
- Bold first-frame claim, cuts every 1-3 s, medium ↔ close-up alternation.
- ALL CAPS heavy captions with the active word in yellow or green.
- Emoji and image pop-ups, dark contrasty grade, pop/whoosh on each pop-up.
- Widely copied: change one thing (font, highlight colour or position).

## Iman Gadzhi premium
- Calm confidence: lowercase captions with weight shifting light → bold.
- Smooth eased zooms (no hard punches), one palette, vintage textures, curated stock.
- Music carries the mood; few subtle SFX.

## MrBeast-lite (stakes)
- State the stakes at once. A counter, timer or progress bar whenever one exists. 3-4-word text.
- Music changes per phase; silence before a result. The 2024+ era is slower and story-first.

## Calm educational
- Fewer cuts with natural pauses kept, hand-drawn labels, lo-fi music barely audible or none.

## Reference library profiles (`pro-video-editing`)
- **P1 glass spotlight motion ad:** 6-10 s, music-led, cut-out subject + glass frame + 3-tier type.
- **P2 editorial red studio:** light studio, giant red italic words, halftone and streak transitions.
- **P3 podcast magazine:** serif italic keywords, B-roll strips.
- **P4 results showcase:** raw-vs-edit shells.
- **P5 red-gradient explainer:** glass UI cards, behind-head words. Its Shorts cut follows the same grammar.

## Borrow without mixing
- Keep one caption system and one transition library per video.
- Borrow devices (a freeze, a counter, a light-rise) only if they fit the chosen style's grammar.
- Make one original change per video ("Claude's idea") so the edit is not a copy.

## P10 and P11 in brief (learned 2026-11-20 from two reference shorts)
| Profile | What it is | Borrow |
|---|---|---|
| **P10 amber UI educator** | 29 s, 9 shots, dark studio, amber accent. Few cuts but text on screen 77% of the time | Typed search/prompt-bar scene to state the topic; one-word light captions on the chest; a flat shape growing BEHIND the speaker to keep a 7 s take alive; a wipe themed to the topic; hook stack in 0.8 s (sticker outline + blur-in word + particles + letter-build title) |
| **P11 podcast scene inserts** | 34.5 s, 20 shots (35 cuts/min), bright podcast set. 60% of words on screen | 5-7 quotable lines each get a full-screen scene with the words INSIDE it (on a desk, a monitor, paper, behind a building, on ribbons); two-size light captions for the rest; palette from the guest's clothes; red only for the negative; big word behind the mic; ends on the payoff word |

Shared by both (so treat as current pro practice, alongside the Tharun grammar):
1. Captions are small, light and lowercase at chest height, not heavy caps in the lower third.
2. A-roll never sits alone for long: a designed scene or an animated background arrives every 1-5 s.
3. Each video has ONE palette and one accent; type styles may vary per scene, colours may not.
4. Masters are -14 LUFS (both measured -14.1 / -14.3), matching Deepak's rule.
5. Speech is airtight: 177-226 words/min, no gap of 0.5 s or more.
Full notes: `pro-video-editing/profiles/P10-amber-ui-educator-tips.md`, `P11-podcast-scene-inserts.md`.

## P12 in brief (learned 2026-11-21 from the "zip 1" analysis pack)
**P12 editorial cutout explainer:** 29.9 s faceless voice-over reel, 10 scenes (average 3.0 s), no footage and no music. Light studio wall with cutouts for the hook, ONE light-leak flash at 37% of runtime, then dark glow scenes built around one object each.

| Borrow | How |
|---|---|
| Rule-of-three hook | Three parallel one-sentence examples on one template, each exactly the same length (80 f). Only the first gets an entrance; 2 and 3 are hard cuts with the subject already in place |
| Stat wedge | Any percentage: grey disc + ghosted logo behind the head; wedge = percent x 3.6°, colour only inside it |
| Blur-in two-font caption | Bold sans line + italic serif line, word by word (blur 14→0 px, 6-8 f). For designed scenes only; never mixed with word-pop captions on the A-roll |
| Object with a state | One literal object per idea; its change is the message (pie fills, lamp turns red → green). Caption colour follows the object; the second caption appears in a new place |
| Caption-free line | One line per short is told by the graphic alone |
| Light-leak flash | 10-12 f at act turns, ends on one scanline frame, same leak each time, 3 at most |
| Other scene changes | Object wipe (6-7 f), one-frame invert before the punchline, zoom-through (8-10 f), re-stage without a cut |
| Sound | Airy whoosh or click on every scene change; ONE sub boom under the biggest reveal; voice only, no music |
| Shape | Punchline is the shortest scene (38 f); endcard is two lines and holds |

Override the reference: keep text inside y 210-1500 (its headline sits at y 150-190); master at -1 dBTP or lower (it peaked at -0.2); credited portraits and checked numbers; proofread (it shipped three typos).
Full notes: `faceless-explainer-reel-edit-style/GUIDE.md` (its own skill folder). Frames and data: `faceless-explainer-reel-edit-style/references/pack/`.

P15 (learned 2026-11-22 from the user's master prompt + one reel): full skill `04-styles/ultimate-editors-shortform-style/GUIDE.md`. Borrow: two-font caption group (small sans + one script keyword), darkened and blurred lower band behind on-screen UI, 58.5 / 41.5 split, off-white grid background, step pill for list videos. Always with `02-craft/beginner-to-pro-edit-tips`.

| Borrow | How |
|---|---|
| Event map | The plan's shot table gets one row per event (`t, VO words, picture / transition, device, camera, text, SFX`); no gap over 1.3 s |
| Two-tier caption (P13) | Small line carries the sentence, one big keyword per group is the reward; keyword colour = role |
| Hidden jump cut (P13) | Push-through (1.0 → 1.26x in 12 f, cut, 1.35 → 1.0x in 16 f), a graphic over the cut, or a new framing of 15 % or more |
| Pedestal list (P13) | One pedestal per item rises in 12-16 f; the icon drops on its keyword; the label is printed on the pedestal |
| Highlighter → circle → punch (P14) | Highlight the headline line by line, circle the number about 10 f after it is spoken, punch in x1.35 on the next clause |
| Text as object (P14) | No captions in a faceless story; a name, date, number or verdict is printed on a surface in the scene |
| Sound grammar | Tick = small event, whoosh-boom = new place, sub boom = meaning word; 5-10 SFX per 10 s |
