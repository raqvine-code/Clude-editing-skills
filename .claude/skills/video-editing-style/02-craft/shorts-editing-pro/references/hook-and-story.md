# Hook and story (9:16)

## Hook (first 0-1.5 s)

**Stack three hooks.** Every reference moves by frame 3 (V).
- **Visual:** a punch-in or push, a flash, a B-roll burst or a giant word.
- **Verbal:** the strongest line, said first.
- **Text:** 1-3 giant words, behind the head or slammed.

**Hook lines that work**, from best to weakest:
1. A command ("Go all in.").
2. A contrarian claim.
3. A number or result.
4. "You're doing X wrong".
5. A question with an obvious open loop.

**Never open with:**
- a greeting, name, logo or "in this video";
- a slow fade-in;
- captions only on a static frame.

**No added hook-title text when Deepak's line is already strong** (`my-editing-rules` section 6). Make the opening visually impactful instead: words he speaks may be designed big; invented slogans may not.

**Metric** (YouTube Studio > Shorts > "viewed vs swiped away"):
- 70 %+ is a good hook, 75-80 % strong. These are unofficial benchmarks (ONL).
- A steep drop in 0-3 s means a hook problem; a slow bleed means a pacing problem.

## Story shapes
| Shape | Beats | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis loop | thesis → reason → example → turn → payoff = thesis again | motivation, advice (Go All In) |
| Problem → fix | pain → why → fix → result | tips, tech |
| List | promise N → items with step scenes → payoff | Tharun reels (list starts at about 12 % of runtime) |
| Story | situation → stake → twist → lesson | personal stories |

- One thought per short. If the raw has two ideas, make two shorts.
- **Open loop:** start a visual or question in the hook and pay it off at the end. Example: the diver frozen mid-air in the hook only splashes at the payoff (18. Go All In).
- **Loop ending:** end on the payoff word, with no outro and no "follow for more". The last frame should flow visually and verbally into frame 0. Since 2025-03-31, Shorts public views count replays (ONL), so loops raise views.
- **CTA:** only if Deepak asks. Use a held card or chip in the last 1.5-2.5 s, or a mid-roll after the 2nd of 3 items (Tharun). Never a subscribe card unless he supplies the button.

## Three-speed rhythm
- **Very fast:** hook and emphasis (4-6 f snaps, 2 f flashes).
- **Medium:** information (cuts every 1-2 s).
- **Slow, but still moving:** an impact or emotional line gets a 2-3 % drift, a freeze or a slow-mo B-roll.

Contrast between these speeds is what makes pace feel high. Constant frantic cutting does not.
