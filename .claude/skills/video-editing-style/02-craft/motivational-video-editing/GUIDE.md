# Motivational video editing

Refs: A = Tharun Speaks (6 habits, maroon); B = maroon-sweater creator (notebook world); C = podcast video (cream + red). Colours judged by eye. Deepak's rules in `mistakes-to-avoid` always win.

## Colour
- One world colour + one accent (A maroon/black/white text; C cream/black/one red; B black-navy + paper with red and navy ink).
- A second colour is only ever a code (B's four sticky squares = steps 1-4; A's green/yellow/red only inside gauges), never decoration.
- Red key word on white text is the cheapest emphasis.
- Next video: choose the accent with Deepak; not violet, not default maroon.

## Type
- Hero word: ultra-condensed heavy white caps behind his head with a tiny line above/below (Impact / Archivo narrow).
- Two voices: bold sans for punch (Tactic Sans / Inter) + serif italic (EB Garamond Italic) for the human line.
- Keyword caption 1-3 bold words; two-line stack = small word over huge word.
- Hand-lettered words and circles only inside paper/notebook scenes.

## Text animation
- Words arrive one by one on black, hold 1-2 s, key word turns accent.
- Lists type onto a notepad one item at a time, icon chips blur in per line.
- Hero word pops from the bottom / punches in on the stressed word, leaves with the sentence.
- Active step grows and glows, others fade. Numbers count up; bars rise with ease-out.

## Pacing
- About one cut per 2.5 s; a quarter of cuts under 1 s apart (montages), rest 3-6 s.
- 0.5 s montage shots (hero words, photos, proof) at the emotional turn, then a long calm hold. Film footage 3-6 s; question on black 2.5 s.
- Nothing static longer than ~5 s while he talks.

## Story
1. Hook in 10 s: hard question or broken belief. 2. Promise the structure ("6 things"). 3. Each point = claim -> one personal story/example -> one-line takeaway (gets the hero word). 4. Breather (plug, black card) every 2-3 points. 5. Recap card, then ONE action. 6. End on a held black card with one line.

## Visual devices
- One returning object carries the video (water tank, coloured squares, lotus). Pick one.
- Numbered chapter cards with counter; recap before the end.
- Paper/notebook scene for steps (hand, markers, circles), PiP of him in the corner.
- Torn paper reveals a chart; floating tiles on black; frosted tags beside the head.
- Sponsor plug keeps the video's look (logo morph, counting stats, "link in description" chip).

## Transitions and SFX
- Hard cut between head and full-screen scene; short B&W/inverted flash; shrink-to-B&W pause; zoom punch on hero word; whip/hand-flip into paper scene. Slow fades only on film footage.
- SFX (inferred): hit per hero word, whoosh on swaps/flips, soft ticks as list items type, riser before chapter card, silence or music drop on last black card. Levels: `sound-and-music-design`.

## B-roll
- Never bare: lowercase word-by-word caption on top, footage dimmed so text reads.
- Match the idea, not the word (night city = ambition, doomscroll = distraction).
- Holds 2-4 s; 0.5 s in montage; epic film footage 3-6 s as punctuation.
- Screen recordings for tool points; archival clips/news as proof with app-screenshot cutaway.
- Same dimmed, slightly desaturated grade across clips. Letterboxed film + slow fades = separate "cinema" mode for story beats only.
- Cut back to him on the takeaway; B-roll never covers the hero word.
- For Deepak: `broll-library` first, 1440p+ only, `png-library` pop-ups for logos/products.

## Do NOT copy
- Colour world behind his cut-out: hero words go on his real wall; full colour worlds only when he is off screen.
- Arrows across footage (only inside full-screen paper scenes); sentence captions (1-4 words only); tilted cards; text under 24 px.
- Theory diagrams for AI concepts: show each concept as a real thing (product screen, example, number, story).

## Plan checklist ("motivation + 5 AI concepts")
Hook + promise -> ONE returning object -> 5 chapters (claim, example, takeaway + hero word) -> breather mid -> recap of 5 -> one-action CTA on black -> check `mistakes-to-avoid` -> 2-3 style frames + sync clip before full render.

## What made the v2 redo work (video 13; v1 rejected as "only text and basic sound effects")
- A built visual answers each spoken idea about every 5 s (UI doing the task, funnel, keycaps, decision gate, orbs, lanes, B&W pause). A caption is never the answer alone.
- Real footage: 19 Pexels slots, 2560x1440, dimmed and captioned; Indian people when the line is about Indian students/teachers/founders; in-points on the word.
- Proof beats: real highlighted news headline; match cut of five real sources.
- Keep two reference systems apart: crimson glass for lists/product, charcoal chalk for ideas.

## Applied: video 13 "Top 5 Skills to Stand Out in the AI Era"
- Returning object = 2026 year grid (365 cells, 75 left): hook, count-down 365 -> 75, "one AI tool a day", "-> 2027", close fills last 75 cells ("DAY 75 / EVERY DAY").
- Hero words printed on his real wall (multiply ink, person matte in front, x1.08 punch-in), ~30 in 12 min, beside the head where mattes sampled every 2 s show free wall; long words fall back to keyword caption.
- Questions arrive word by word on black; ONE held black card "See you in 75 days." with silence.
- Many outputs -> one choice: prompt bar + dots counting 100 -> 1,000 + one red dot "1 / TASTE" (100 fake thumbnails was rejected, `mistakes-to-avoid` 10b).
- Full-screen scenes hard-cut in/out while he keeps talking; a change every 4-6 s, never a gap over ~6 s without a beat.
