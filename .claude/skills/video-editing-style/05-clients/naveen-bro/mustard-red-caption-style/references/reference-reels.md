## Search notes
- Web search (2026-11-25) found no source naming "mustard" captions. Two useful notes: red vibrates against skin tones and suffers in Instagram compression ([blitzcutai](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-colors-instagram-reels-2026)); the "vintage yellow subtitle" look is yellow sans with a thin dark outline ([Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-use-the-yellow-subtitle-font-you-see-in-movies/)).
- None of the references uses mustard AND red together on one caption. The pairing (mustard keyword, brick-red negative word) is my synthesis from R1 + R3 + R4 for the client's request, marked **Claude** in GUIDE.md.
- **Instagram in the built-in browser (signed in, view only):** keyword search returned results only for some queries ("aesthetic subtitles reels" yes; "caption style edit", "podcast editing", "yellow and red captions editing" no). With the pane hidden the grid images do not lazy-load and reels play at 360x640, and the reels feed jumps to other posts. Faster next time: the user screen-records the Instagram reels they like into `edits/mustard-red-captions/inbox/`, then run `06-tools/watch`.
- **YouTube frame capture that works (no download):** on a `/shorts/<id>` page, take `ytd-reel-video-renderer[is-active] video`, seek, `drawImage` into a canvas (not tainted: the video is fed through MSE), lay the canvases out in a fixed overlay grid and screenshot; `getImageData` gives the text colours. Video thumbnails `i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/hq1|hq2|hq3.jpg` are frames from 25/50/75 % of the video: a quick screening grid for many results at once.

Trend notes (web, 2026, vendor blogs: directional only): "documentary realism" and "phone-first cinematic" are the aesthetic lanes for talking heads: one grade across the whole video, light grain, slow moves, captions only where needed ([OpusClip, 11 editing aesthetics 2026](https://www.opus.pro/blog/editing-aesthetics-dominating-short-form-2026)). Serifs are back; Instrument Serif is now so common designers call it lazy ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/fonts-typography/serif-fonts-are-back-in-fashion-and-i-couldnt-be-happier)); "modern retro serif" is a 2026 microtrend ([Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/microtrend-modern-retro-serif)); Fraunces is the flexible free editorial serif (weight, optical size, SOFT, WONK axes) ([Made Good Designs](https://madegooddesigns.com/trending-canva-fonts-2026/)). Montserrat Bold stays the most recommended caption sans; Playfair large and sparingly ([BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-reels-2026)).
