# Faceless explainer reel edit style (editorial cutout explainer, profile P12)

Learned 2026-11-21 from the "zip 1" analysis pack. This folder is the home of the style; `pro-video-editing/profiles/P12-editorial-cutout-explainer.md` only points here.

## When to choose this style
| The video is... | Use |
|---|---|
| A voice-over or script with no camera footage; "X owns Y%" facts; finance, business or money idea; product or template promo | **This skill, full build** (section 12 A) |
| A talking-head short that has a percentage, a stop/start or myth/fact line, or needs a strong act change | Keep the chosen talking-head style and **borrow devices from here** as scene inserts (section 12 B) |
| Energetic talking-head advice | `tharun-speaks-edit-style` (default), not this |
| Seated educator tips or a podcast clip | `pro-video-editing` P10 / P11, not this |
| Music-led 6-10 s ad | `pro-video-editing` P1 / P2, not this |

Read with: `shorts-editing-pro` (entry point for every short, hard rules and safe zones), `fonts-and-colours`, `mistakes-to-avoid`. Frames to look at before a build: `references/pack/analysis/key_stills/` (the looks) and `references/pack/analysis/detail_frames/` (frame-numbered animation sheets, listed in `references/pack/pack-README.md`).

Ref: "zip 1" analysis pack, learned 2026-11-21. One 29.9 s finance reel (720x1280, 30 fps, 896 frames, 10 scenes, -14.2 LUFS, true peak -0.2 dBTP, LRA 3.2). The pack held no video: a ready skill file, 3 fps sheets of the whole reel, 13 frame-numbered sheets, 3 stills, word-timed transcript, scene scores and audio band data. All of it is kept in `references/pack/` (`pack-skill-as-received.md` is the skill as it arrived; `analysis/` holds the sheets, stills, transcript and audio data).

Labels: **(M)** measured in the pack data or counted on the frame-numbered sheets · **(O)** seen on the sheets · **(E)** estimate (font names, px sizes and blur radii are scaled from 720p to 1080x1920) · **(S)** suggested for our edits, not tested yet.

Use for: a voice-over or script with no camera footage; "X owns Y%" fact reels; finance, business and money ideas; a product or template promo. Study only: never reuse the reference's cutouts, art or script.

## 1. What makes it this style
1. **No footage.** Every scene is built: one cutout subject or one 3D object + a caption + one graphic. (O)
2. **Light act, then dark act.** Off-white studio wall for the hook (0-37% of runtime), one flash, then near-black to the end. The flip happens once. (M)
3. **Two-font captions.** A bold sans line + a thin italic serif line, revealed word by word with a soft blur-in. No boxes, strokes, emoji or bounce. (O)
4. **One accent hue per scene.** Everything else is black, white and grey. (O)
5. **Nothing is still.** Only 4 of 896 frames had no motion (pack count). Everything eases in, then keeps drifting. (M)
6. **Every scene change is designed and has a sound.** Plain hard cuts only between repeats of the same template. (M)

## 3. Script shape (reuse the shape, write new words)
| % of runtime | Beat | Look |
|---|---|---|
| 0-27 | Three parallel examples, one sentence each, same length | Light |
| 27-37 | Payoff that joins the three, then a 3-4 word pivot line | Light |
| 37-50 | The insight, with one metaphor object | Dark |
| 50-61 | The consequence ("if you... you'll...") | Dark, B&W |
| 61-74 | Contrast pair: stop X / start Y | Dark, red then green |
| 74-78 | Punchline | Dark, gold |
| 78-94 | CTA with product objects | Dark, brand hue |
| 94-100 | Endcard, two lines | Dark |

- Voice: 88 spoken words in 29.5 s = about 179 wpm overall (counted from the pack transcript; the pack's own note says about 160). Short declarative sentences. 11 gaps between sentences, each 0.30-0.60 s. (M)
- Captions are a **condensed** copy of the voice: "And yet" and "Because" are dropped. About 9 words in 10 are on screen. (M)
- **One line is carried by the graphic alone** (the pie growing). Plan one such line per reel. (O)

## 4. The two looks
**Light (hook)**
- Wall `#F3F3F3` to `#EDEDED`; faint contour lines (about 6% black) and tiny dots that drift slowly. Soft inner shadow at the frame edge so the frame reads as a card. (M colours, O rest)
- Cutout centred, filling the lower 65%. Soft shadow on the wall, down-right. The bottom of the body melts into blur, never a hard crop. (O)
- Text near-black `#2A2829`, top left, left margin about 13% of width. (M)
- **Safe-zone fix (S):** the reference sets the headline at 8-10% of height (y 150-190), inside our unsafe top band. Put it at y 230 or lower.

**Dark (body and CTA)**
- Background `#030303` to `#171717` with ONE texture: rounded-diamond hairlines (about 8% white), black marble, or HUD micro-text rotated 90° at the side edges. (M colours, O rest)
- One hero object, lit from inside or rim-lit, with bloom in its own colour on the background. (O)
- White text with a soft outer glow; accent captions take the scene hue. Optional giant ghost word behind the subject (vertical caps, about 10% opacity). (O)

**Both:** the outer 10-15% of the frame is slightly out of focus, motion blur on every moving layer, subject always layered above its graphic. (O)

## 5. Type
| Role | Reference look (E) | Our stand-in (S, from `fonts-and-colours`) |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | Tight bold grotesk, tracking -20 to -40 | Inter Tight Bold or Poppins 700 |
| Emphasis | High-contrast italic serif | Playfair Display Italic or EB Garamond Italic |
| Reflective lines, endcard | Geometric sans Light + SemiBold | Poppins 300 + 600 |
| CTA label | Condensed caps, gradient fill + glow, one line only | Bebas Neue |

- Every caption: **2 fonts, at most 2 lines, 2-6 words per line.** Line 1 sans, line 2 italic serif (or the serif word inline: "Name *owns*"). On the contrast pair the order flips: italic first, bold second. (O)
- Sizes at 1080x1920 (E): light headline about 66 px, stat sub-line about 40 px, dark captions 84-90 px, endcard about 76 px. Line width 55-65% of the frame, never over 70%. Tight leading (1.0-1.1).
- Sentence case; italic lines may be all lowercase.
- The reference shipped three errors on screen ("Billionairs", "After Effect Users", "this project files is"). Proofread every caption.

## 6. Text animation
- **Blur-in word reveal (about 90% of the text):** each word goes opacity 0 to 100, blur about 14 px to 0, y +10 px to 0, grey to its final colour, in 6-8 f, ease-out. Words never scale or bounce. (O; px values E)
- **Random character fade (once, the pivot line):** letters appear in random order over about 12 f. (M f312-323)
- **Exit:** the block fades with a slight blur in 8-12 f, or leaves with the transition. (M)
- **Line swap inside a scene:** about 12-24 f overlap; the old line dims and blurs back, the new one reveals on top. (M f501-525)
- **Glow** ramps up with the reveal on dark scenes. (O)

**Timing against the voice (M: frames vs the pack transcript, which is ASR and can be 0.1-0.3 s off):**
- Hook words land within about 5 f of the spoken word, at uneven gaps that follow the voice (6, 8, 18, 8, 6 f).
- Right after a flash the first word shows 6-10 f late (the scene must clear first); the line catches up within 3-4 words.
- In dark scenes words step at an even 4 f, so the last words of a line can sit up to about 0.35 s ahead of the voice.
- The punchline cut lands about 10 f after its sentence starts: the voice leads the picture.
- **Our rule stays (S):** hero words go on the voice or up to 0.3 s early, checked against the voice level. Copy the even 4 f step only for the glue words between them.

## 7. Transitions (9 scene changes in 30 s)
| Transition | Count | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| **Light-leak flash** (the signature) | 3 | 10-12 f. A bloom with a thin horizontal streak is seeded on the hero object 1-2 f early. A white core fills the frame with rose `#E89898` and slate-indigo `#686888` soft edges and a fine vertical line texture (fluted glass) on the leading edge. The new scene ghosts through. **The last frame is the new scene under horizontal scanlines for 1 f**, then clean. Used at act changes; the same leak every time. (M f331-340, f444-455, f704-714) |
| Hard cut | 2 | Only between repeats of the same template. (M f80, f160) |
| Re-stage (no cut) | 1 | Fade text and graphic 12 f, scale the hero to 85% and lower it, slide the others in from the edges in about 14-20 f with motion blur. (M f234-266) |
| Object wipe | 1 | A foreground object fills the lens for 6-7 f; cut under it. (M f541-547) |
| Invert | 1 | ONE frame negative of the outgoing picture, then hard cut. Before the punchline. (M f665) |
| Zoom-through | 1 | A scene object flies into the camera over 8-10 f and covers the frame. (M f838-848) |

## 8. Motion rules
- **Entrance:** subject or object rises from below the frame, expo ease-out: about 70% of the travel in the first 20 f, about 66 f in total, then a creep until the cut. The headline drifts up 5-7% of frame height with it. (M f10-79)
- **Template repeats:** no entrance; only text and graphic re-animate. (M)
- **Camera:** slow push on every scene (1-2% scale per second). One lateral pan (about 40% of frame width, about 36 f, ease in-out) to reveal a bigger object. (O; pan M f407-443)
- **Foreground debris** (bills) crosses in front of the text, out of focus, at 2-3 depths. (O)
- **Line art** (hand-drawn pointers, dotted connector, dotted ellipse) draws on over 10-24 f with the caption it belongs to. (M f316-330, f561-581, f609-633)

## 9. Graphic devices
- **Stat wedge:** light-grey disc (diameter about 50% of frame width) behind the head, logo ghosted in grey. A wedge wipes clockwise from 12 o'clock to exactly `stat% x 3.6°` (20% = 72°) in about 34 f. Inside the wedge the logo shows in brand colour. (M f24-58; angle read on the stills)
- **Metaphor object with a state:** one literal object per idea (pie = share, traffic light = stop/start, statue = wealth, bills = money). Its change carries the meaning: the wedge fills, one lamp dims as the other brightens, bills rain. (O)
- **State-linked caption:** caption colour and scene tint follow the object. The second caption appears in a different place from the first. (O)
- **CTA objects:** two 3D icons in opposite corners, slow rotation, slightly out of focus; one of them becomes the zoom-through. (O)

## 10. Colour (M, sampled by the pack)
- Light act: median luma about 80%, very low saturation (only skin and the wedge carry colour). Dark act: median luma about 3%, highlights to about 85%, blacks never lifted.
- Accents: amber `#E48424` / `#FCB46C`, lamp red `#FC0C0C`, lamp green `#54E40C`, periwinkle `#846CCC` (text `#B8A8F8`), muted gold `#6C5424`, blue-grey wedge `#A8B8C8`. Caption tints are lighter versions of the lamp colour, with white on the bold line.
- Statues and money are fully B&W. No film grain, no LUT cast.

## 11. Sound (M: measured from the audio, nobody listened)
- **The voice is the bed.** Deep, close, heavily compressed. -14.2 LUFS, LRA 3.2 LU, near mono (side/mid 0.127).
- **No audible music.** Between sentences the mids and highs fall to the noise floor; no steady tempo was found.
- **A short airy sound on every scene change** (6-16 kHz, 100-200 ms): all 9 changes have one within 0.2 s, and so does the red-to-green lamp switch. About 30 such sounds in 30 s, so some also sit on word reveals and wedge wipes.
- **One sub boom** (20-80 Hz, about 1.3 s, about 20 dB above the bed) at 13.9-15.2 s: under the pan to the bigger pie and into flash 2. Used once.
- True peak was -0.2 dBTP. Do not copy that: our master is -14 LUFS with -1 dBTP or lower.

## 12. Using it in our edits (S)
**A. Full P12 build.** Pick it when there is a voice-over and no camera footage, or Deepak asks for a faceless or pure motion-graphics reel.
1. Write to the beat table in section 3. Mark the pivot line, the punchline and the one caption-free line.
2. Voice first; trim sentence gaps to 0.3-0.6 s; get word times.
3. Assets: cutouts, logos in grey and in colour, one object per dark scene, textures, one light-leak clip, whoosh / click / boom.
4. Build the light template once and repeat it; build each dark scene around its object.
5. Animate in this order: entrance, graphic state, word reveals, drift on everything, foreground debris. Then transitions, then one SFX per transition.

**B. Borrow into a talking-head short** (the usual case). His A-roll is the "light act"; P12 dark object scenes become the 1.3-4 s designed inserts on key lines (the 28-41% scene-insert share from P10/P11).
- Any percentage: the stat wedge.
- Any contrast pair (stop/start, myth/fact, before/after): one object that changes state, caption colour follows.
- Act turns: the light-leak flash in place of the white flash, 3 at most per short, same asset each time.
- One caption-free line; one sub boom under the biggest reveal; punchline scene the shortest.

**Hard rules that override the reference**
- One caption system on the A-roll. The blur-in two-font model lives inside designed scenes or a full P12 build; it never mixes with word-pop captions over his face.
- Real people named on screen need a credited portrait (public domain or CC); real logos only, no lookalikes (`mistakes-to-avoid`, `my-editing-rules`).
- Never invent a number. Re-check every stat before it goes on screen (`text-highlight-match-cut`). The reference's own figures were not checked here.
- Safe zone: text inside x 90-960, y 210-1500.

**Kit status: not tested.** `shortkit.py` has hard-pop captions, giants, B-roll slots and 3-frame white / negative flashes. It has no blur-in reveal, no two-font line, no wedge and no light leak. Plan (S): render P12 scenes as clips (PIL + OpenCV, as `edits/telugu1/tools/scenes.py` did) and drop them in as B-roll slots with `entry: "cut"`; pre-render the leak as an 11-frame overlay screened across the cut. Sketches:
- Word reveal: per word, alpha 0 to 1, Gaussian blur 14 px to 0, y +10 px to 0 over 7 f, ease-out.
- Wedge: PIL `pieslice` from -90° to `-90 + 3.6 x pct` as a mask for the colour logo over the grey logo; animate the end angle over 34 f.
- Invert: `255 - frame` for exactly 1 f (shortkit's "neg" flash is 3 f).
The first edit that uses P12 logs what worked in `shorts-editing-pro/LEARNING-LOG.md`.

## 13. QC
- [ ] Something moves by frame 3-4; first word on screen within 0.15 s.
- [ ] Each caption: 2 fonts, 2 lines or fewer, hero words on the voice.
- [ ] Wedge angle equals the stated percentage; colour only inside the wedge.
- [ ] No frozen scene: drift on background, subject and text.
- [ ] One accent hue per scene; the light-to-dark flip happens once.
- [ ] Every scene change has a transition from section 7 and a sound.
- [ ] Light-leak flash is 12 f or shorter and ends on one scanline frame.
- [ ] Punchline is the shortest scene; endcard holds 1 s or more after its last word.
- [ ] Spelling checked on every caption; text inside the safe zone.
- [ ] -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP or lower.

## Do not copy
The reference's celebrity cutouts, its pie and statue art, its script and figures, its seller CTA, and its typos. Not this style: talking-head jump cuts, boxed or karaoke captions, emoji, stock B-roll montage, beat-synced music cuts, two accent hues in one scene, static holds.
