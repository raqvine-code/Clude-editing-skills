# Short-form editing: online research (2026-11-15)

Label **ONL** = from public web sources (mostly creator/vendor blogs, not platform specs or peer-reviewed studies). Use as guidance; when it conflicts with a V finding from the user's references, the reference wins for style, ONL wins only for platform mechanics (safe zones, loudness).

## Hook (first 1-3 s) - ONL, agrees with references (V: every reference moves by frame 3)
- Open with a visual interrupt + a specific verbal promise naming the viewer's problem/outcome. Say what they get in the first ~2 s.
- Hook length ~1.5-3 s with quick cuts; no logo/intro slate before the hook.
- The whole arc (hook -> build -> payoff) matters, not only the first 3 s.
- Diagnose with the retention graph: a steep drop in 0-3 s = hook problem.

## Length & pacing - ONL
- 15-30 s is the common sweet spot; a tight 30 s with high completion beats a loose 60 s.
- Cut dead air and slow sections aggressively; speed up slow bits.

## Captions - ONL (direction reliable, numbers not)
- Large share of short-form is watched muted (estimates 50-85 %, inconsistent). Always burn in captions.
- Word-by-word / phrase-timed captions with emphasis on keywords outperform static full-sentence subtitles in vendor tests. Matches V references (keyword tiers, neon pills, underline progress).

## Safe zones for 1080x1920 (Reels/Shorts/TikTok) - ONL, numbers vary by source
**Superseded 2026-11-25** by `pro-font-and-subtitle-guide/references/vertical-text-placement-research.md`: text inside x 120-960, y 270-1470; subtitle block centre at 58-65 % of the height, under the chin. The numbers below are kept for history.

- Keep text and faces out of: top ~210 px, bottom ~340-480 px, right ~84-120 px (buttons).
- Conservative central safe area ~996x1400 px. Captions above ~450 px from the bottom.
- `qc.md` rule (14 % bottom / 8 % top / 12 % right) is consistent with this; prefer the stricter bottom (25 %) when the platform caption text is long.

## Audio - ONL
- Master -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP (YouTube normalizes louder files down; Reels similar). Some creators push -10..-12 for in-feed punch; references measured -16.6..-12.4 LUFS (V). User rule: -14.
- Slight mid-high presence lift helps phone speakers. Subtle SFX for emphasis.
