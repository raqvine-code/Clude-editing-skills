# P5 Red Gradient YouTube Explainer (somratdutta and similar creator-educator channels)

Refs: 1ef4cbf46f08, cab9807914a3, 7a74184fe0d2, 94a17cc80891 (all V on key moments); screenshots 31-38. All are 16:9 long-form excerpts posted as Shorts (letterboxed with a CTA in the bottom bar). Use for: talking-head education/tech/AI videos in 16:9, and their Shorts cut-downs.

## Look
- Talking head in a dark warm room (practical lamps, bookshelf, guitar), shallow depth; sometimes B&W background with colour subject.
- Graphic world = **red radial gradient** (#2A0003 edge -> #C4161C centre glow) or black with red swoosh glow at a corner.
- **Glass UI cards**: dark-red translucent rounded rectangles with thin light rim, inner gradient, small white UI text; carousels with < > chevrons; node graphs with connector lines; "Tool A / Tool B" tiles.
- Type: clean grotesk (Inter/SF/Helvetica Now) white; giant bold words behind head (ACHIEVABLE, SMARTER, "20s"); outline+fill word pairs ("Video Editing"); red accent numerals ("7 Step Roadmap").
- Lower-third caption: small white sentence with **red underline that grows with the sentence** (94a1).
- Screen recordings framed as cards (tilted or flat) next to a talking-head PIP card with red glow.
- Shorts wrapper: letterbox bars, top pill "Comment 'node' for full video" or bottom "watch full video here" / "Checkout Full Video!" with hand-drawn curved arrow, "full video" highlighted yellow.

## Motion recipe (V)
- Open: frame shrinks into a rounded card (~1.3 s, ease-in-out) revealing gradient; or shot shrinks to card and the canvas pans sideways (~1.3 s) to the next graphic.
- Behind-head word: on in 2 f, then a scale punch-in ~1.1x with motion blur over ~10 f.
- Side panel: slides from right 8 f; bullets type word by word as each point is spoken.
- Whip: horizontal motion blur 4 f between worlds.
- Morph: bar grows into card ~15 f; nodes: card bump + branch slide-out + line draw ~15 f.
- Hexagon mosaic transition ~0.4 s for location changes.
- Title slate: feathered L->R mask reveal with per-letter blur ~1 s.
- Number counter rolls to the target over ~0.5-1 s (S).

## Pacing (V)
- Speech 190-213 wpm. Visual change every 1-3 s but long talking-head holds (up to 10 s) are allowed when a side panel or caption is building.
- One phrase per beat on text-only slides ("no rich parents" / "no cheat codes").
