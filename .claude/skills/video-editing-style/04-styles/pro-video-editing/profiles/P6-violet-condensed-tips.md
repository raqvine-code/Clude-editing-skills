# P6 Violet Condensed Tips (numbered talking-head tips, "edited in just Premiere Pro")

Ref: dd0713672607 (45.2 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, -14.3 LUFS, 8 picture cuts, mean shot 5.65 s, but in-frame motion every 1-2 s) (V). Seen on a 1 fps sheet + close-ups (S).
Use for: "3 mistakes / 3 tips" talking heads, educator or editor-to-editor advice, podcast-mic setups.

## Look
- **Grade:** whole plate pushed to deep blue-violet (~#3A22D8 shadows, lavender highlights); BEFORE card is plain B&W. One hue + white.
- **Giant word behind the head:** condensed sans (Bebas/Anton/Oswald-Compressed class), white with a top-to-bottom fade into the violet, fills the top ~45 % of frame, head occludes it (needs a person matte). Small all-caps lead line above ("IF YOU LOOK AT YOUR", "JUST MAKE SURE TO").
- **Caption:** two tiers on the chest band: small regular lowercase ("you  think") over condensed bold white caps ("THAT LOOKS NOTHING"); 1 short phrase at a time.
- **Micro stickers** hooked onto the giant word: magnifier on "EDITS", lightbulb, red pin, brain emoji on "REMEMBER" (they pop 2-4 f after the word lands).
- **Chapter cards** (full-frame cut to a white page with a violet radial vignette at the edges): giant gradient numeral "1/2/3" + small "lack of / bad" + condensed violet "DIRECTION / DESIGN / POST PRODUCTION" + a small rounded icon badge, long soft drop shadow down-right. Cut in with a white flash.
- **3D object beat:** isometric violet clay object (a vault for "inspiration vault") enters from the left edge on the white page, thin curved guide lines, condensed label right.
- **List pills:** violet translucent glass pills with a dot bullet ("Imbalance scene. / Bad contrast. / Uncommon pattern."), stacked on the chest band, each lands on its word.
- **Counting beat:** three white "?" circle icons pop in a row on the chest for "three things", again at the recap.
- White UI list cards (fake settings rows) slide in as a full-frame insert for "effects like vignettes, blurs...".

## Rhythm (V/S)
Speaker plate stays long (4-10 s) but something new lands every 1-2 s; chapter card = the pattern break per point (~every 13 s). Recap reuses the opening "???" motif (callback).

## Build notes (E)
Matte the person (rembg / u2net_human_seg) for the behind-head word. Fade mask on the giant word: linear-gradient alpha 100 % -> 0 % over its bottom third. Chapter card = 0.2 s white flash in, 0.4 s scale 1.04 -> 1.0 settle. Keep stickers 1 per word, never on the face.
