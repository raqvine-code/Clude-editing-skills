Refs: 91170c08735e (V), 28d9fc8134d2 (S), 60a96c2030c4 (S, poster variant); screenshots 12, 19-22, 27. Use for: podcast clips, interview quotes, motivational VO with stock/AI imagery.

## Look
- The 9:16 frame is treated as a **magazine page**: maroon (#6E0A1E-ish) or cream ruled paper background, thin top category labels in spaced small caps (DREAM / SACRIFICE / ACHIEVE; MISTAKES / HARDWORK / CONSISTENCY).
- Speaker cut-out or framed photo on the page; name lower-third with red bar ("RAJ SHAMANI / INDIAN PODCASTER").
- Caption system: small sans lead words + giant **serif italic display** word (Playfair/Didot-like) for the emotional keyword; for B-roll, heavy caps with coloured highlight box (green, red).
- B-roll inserts: cinematic film clips, teal/orange grade, stacked 2-3 horizontal strips, **red rectangle eye-bars** (censor look), crop-mark corners.
- Graphic devices: tree/bracket lines that draw then words land at ends; red circle behind head; giant repeated outlined name type behind subject (Manny Mann); red sticky-note tags; textured paper (kraft, blue grunge) for 1-word slides; 3D object on black.
- Poster variant (storyboard reel): monochrome dark posters, stencil/pixel caps, serif + script pairs, smoke and lightning overlays.

## Motion recipe (V where framed)
- Caption build: lead fades in, display word fades/scale 95->100 over ~5 f, lands 0.2-0.4 s before the spoken keyword.
- Phrase change on B-roll: white full-height bar wipe 3 f reveals next phrase, highlight box grows L->R 3 f.
- Cuts inside the page every 1.6-2.1 s (median shot 1.6-1.9 s in the card).
- Ends on black card "edited by NIKHIL" with glow.

## Rules
- Hinglish speech: captions keep original language; the emotional keyword (Sacrifice, Depend, Difference) is the only big word in a line.
- Never cover the speaker's eyes with type; eye-bars only on B-roll strangers/actors.
