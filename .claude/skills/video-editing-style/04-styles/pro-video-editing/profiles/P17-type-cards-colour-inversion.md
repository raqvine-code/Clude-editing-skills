# P17 Type cards + colour inversion

**This profile lives in its own skill: `04-styles/editor-showcase-caption-edit-style/GUIDE.md`** (profile table in section 3, recipes in section 4). Measurements: its `references/reel-notes-R5-R8.md`; frame boards: its `references/frames/`.

In one paragraph: a black-and-white talking head that hands the screen to full-frame type cards in ONE colour and its inverse (deep green / white), a plate change every 0.83 s, 72 % of the runtime on cards. One heavy grotesk hero word 76-87 % of the width with tiny light lead-ins; hard colour inversion on the stressed word; ghost-to-solid arrivals; every return to the speaker is a focus pull; the speaker or the live video sits inside a rounded card or a phone mock-up. Reference R5 (13.5 s).

Build: `04-styles/editor-showcase-caption-edit-style/tools/caption_moves.py` renders the write-on, pills, converge-in, type cards and tiles as `layers` for shortkit. Client rules (fonts, colours, no BGM, no grade) win over the profile: borrow the move, keep the client's type.
