## What to borrow
1. Long take + animated background behind the speaker instead of more cuts.
2. A transition themed to the topic (paint wipe for "color key") instead of a stock whoosh cut.
3. A typed search/prompt-bar scene to announce the topic of an AI or software video.
4. One-word light captions on the chest for a calm, premium feel; pair with ONE over-designed word per video.
5. Hook stack inside 0.8 s: outline + blur-in word + particles + letter-build title.

## Do not copy
The creator's hat/room identity, the Uzbek script, the exact 3D landscape plate, and the brand UI of any real product (rebuild a generic prompt bar).
