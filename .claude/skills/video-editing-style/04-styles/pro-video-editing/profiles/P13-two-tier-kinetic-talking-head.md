# P13 Two-Tier Kinetic Talking Head

**This profile lives in its own skill: `02-craft/event-map-reel-edit-style/GUIDE.md` (section 5).** Full recipes in its `references/format-b-talking-head.md`, `references/transitions.md`, `references/devices.md`; frames in `references/pack/analysis/R1-*` and `R4-*`.

In one paragraph: a 9:16 talking-head reel (204-224 wpm) where the edit carries the pace. Small sentence-case caption line + ONE big keyword per group on the chest, keyword coloured by role (gold topic, red threat, white + orange bar pivot); three modes switched about every 2.5 s in the breaths (speaker + captions, speaker composited into graphics, full-screen metaphor cutaway); jump cuts hidden under push-through moves, graphics or new framings; lists as pedestals or zoom-bumps; a luma flip or light-leak at the turn; about 9 SFX per 10 s.
