- **Word icons:** COOLING (blue snowflake circle) and HEATING (orange flame circle) pop on their words.
- **Icon halo:** 8 orange circle icons on a thin arc around the speaker's head on "all the different components".
- Palette: warm orange #F28C1E / gold #E9B04A + white + B&W stock. Accent consistent across every graphic.

## Rhythm (V)
A new picture every 1.7-2 s for 15 s: speaker -> split -> B-roll -> B-roll -> speaker -> card -> card -> speaker -> B-roll x2 -> icons -> speaker + halo. Speaker returns every 2-3 shots so the face anchors trust.
