# P7 Noir Red Kinetic (brand/agency talking head, "clean text animation, detailed sound")

Ref: 48a38ca593e4 (22.9 s, 1080x1920, 29.83 fps, -14.6 LUFS, peak -0.6 dBFS, cuts at 3.52 / 7.31 / 7.58 / 11.43-12.04 (4 quick) / 16.06 / 16.26) (V); creator "Hossein", client brand BONOMI. Sheets + close-ups (S).
Use for: B2B / marketing / agency opinion clips, dark moody interiors, serious tone.

## Look
- **Persistent brand bug:** "B●NOMI" wordmark (red dot as the O) bottom centre, small, the whole video; end card = black with the logo flickering in (last ~3 s). Agency rule: client logo on every frame + logo outro.
- **Giant red condensed word behind the subject** ("PRESENCE") with a dark extrude/shadow, on a red-lit wall shot.
- **Editorial grid moment:** B&W tight crop, white matte stroke traced around the subject's silhouette + thin white guide lines (a layout grid), sans words stacking one per spoken word ("means / every / impression").
- **Stat slam:** cut to black, red glowing condensed number with bloom; second line outline red ("OF PURCHASES"). NOTE: the reference wrote "%94" (RTL habit); in English always write "94%".
- **Grain/static dissolve:** a white noise-particle dissolve carries the cut into "MULTIPLE touchpoints" (gold serif italic + outline).
- **Mixed-type stack** on the wall: bold white sans "Organic" + outline gold serif caps "WARMS" + small teal "the audience". Three faces, three weights, still one palette (red/gold/teal on near-black).
- **B&W punch:** desaturated + darker vignette on the punchline sentence, then back to colour.
- Small stacked caps top-left ("ADS / THE / AUDIENCE") for the reply line.

## Rhythm (V)
Mostly one static camera; 4 cuts in 0.6 s around 11.4-12.0 s (stutter-cut energy burst on the stat), then calmer. Text appears word by word on the wall, 0.2-0.4 s apart. Sound design is the selling point ("detailed sound design"): a whoosh/hit on every type entrance, riser into the stat.

## Build notes (E)
Silhouette stroke = dilate the person matte 6-8 px, subtract the matte, fill white. Grain dissolve = threshold a moving noise texture to wipe A->B over 8-10 frames. Keep each type stack in the empty wall area; never on the face.
