# P12 Editorial Cutout Explainer (faceless voice-over reel)

**This profile lives in its own skill: `04-styles/faceless-explainer-reel-edit-style/GUIDE.md`.** Read that file; frames and data are in its `references/pack/`.

In one paragraph: a 9:16 voice-over reel with no camera footage. Cutout subjects on an off-white studio wall for a rule-of-three hook, one light-leak flash at about 37% of runtime, then dark glow scenes built around one object each. Blur-in two-font captions (bold sans + italic serif), logo stat wedge (percent x 3.6°), object-with-a-state for contrast pairs, one caption-free line, a sound on every scene change, one sub boom, no music. Learned 2026-11-21 from the "zip 1" analysis pack (29.9 s, 720x1280, 30 fps, 10 scenes, -14.2 LUFS).
