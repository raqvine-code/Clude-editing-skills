# P20 Script word + small sans on the chest

**This profile lives in its own skill: `04-styles/editor-showcase-caption-edit-style/GUIDE.md`** (profile table in section 3, recipes in section 4). Measurements: its `references/reel-notes-R5-R8.md`; frame boards: its `references/frames/`.

In one paragraph: a calm talking head carried by one caption device: a thin flowing script phrase (50-58 % of the width) that writes on left to right through a blur in 0.4-0.5 s, with tiny clean sans words tucked into it, block centre 58.5 % of the height, cleared in one frame, 0.26-0.77 s of empty screen between groups. Plain statements switch to one line of bold sans. A numbered-tile list device (1-2-3 diamonds, tile 1 rolls to its word), one glass icon, a quiet boxed-subtitle ending. Reference R8 (22.1 s). Nearest older style: P15.

Build: `04-styles/editor-showcase-caption-edit-style/tools/caption_moves.py` renders the write-on, pills, converge-in, type cards and tiles as `layers` for shortkit. Client rules (fonts, colours, no BGM, no grade) win over the profile: borrow the move, keep the client's type.
