# P19 Under-chin kinetic captions in one focal zone

**This profile lives in its own skill: `04-styles/editor-showcase-caption-edit-style/GUIDE.md`** (profile table in section 3, recipes in section 4). Measurements: its `references/reel-notes-R5-R8.md`; frame boards: its `references/frames/`.

In one paragraph: captions live under the chin for the whole video: block centre 53.8 % of the height, 17 % below the chin, 96 % of caption frames inside x 30-70 %, y 45-70 %, eye line fixed through five framings. Bold tight sans keyword in one accent colour (72-80 % of the width) + small dimmed words; keywords blur in over 4-5 frames; one device-card scene; one deliberate scatter on the line about distraction; a middle-box overlay. Reference R7 (31.7 s). The safest base for a 45-60 s client short.

Build: `04-styles/editor-showcase-caption-edit-style/tools/caption_moves.py` renders the write-on, pills, converge-in, type cards and tiles as `layers` for shortkit. Client rules (fonts, colours, no BGM, no grade) win over the profile: borrow the move, keep the client's type.
