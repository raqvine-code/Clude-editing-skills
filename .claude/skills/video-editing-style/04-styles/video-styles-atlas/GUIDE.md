# Video styles atlas (2026-11-25)

**Why it exists:** the user asked to learn every editing style and aesthetic, so every edit picks the right one without being asked (CLAUDE.md Rules 2 and 3). The deep styles already live in their own skills; this atlas maps each video form to its style and holds the forms that had no skill. Fonts for each form: `pro-font-and-subtitle-guide` section 2.

**How to use:** analyse the footage (Rule 2), find the row whose form and goal match, read the linked skill(s), and write the row's numbers into PLAN.md as QC targets. One row per video. Mixing rows only when the user asks.

## 2. Vlog / lifestyle (new)
- **Structure:** a hook (the best moment or the question of the day) in 0-5 s, then a day or journey told in chapters with location title cards. End on a reflection line, not a fade.
- **Rhythm:** B-roll-led montages cut to the music beat (shots 0.5-1.5 s) alternate with talking-to-camera holds (3-8 s), and jump cuts remove every pause over 0.3 s. A J-cut (the next scene's sound starts first) leads into each new location.
- **Look:** one consistent warm natural grade, light grain. Speed ramps (normal to slow) only on 1-2 moments per chapter. Slow motion alternates with real speed, never stacks.
- **Type:** location titles in Bebas Neue tracked wide + small Inter caps (place, time); handwritten notes in Caveat; captions small (Poppins 600 / Bricolage 600).
- **Sound:** licensed music beds that change per chapter, natural sound kept under (footsteps, street, laughter), whooshes only on whips.
- **Shorts version:** the single best 20-40 s moment, the hook in the first frame, captions on.

## 3. Cinematic brand film / wedding highlight (new)
- **Structure:** two-part music arc: slow, emotional intro + ceremony (or story) on song 1, then energy (reception, product in action) on song 2. Vows, speeches or interview lines layered under the score as the story spine. The highlight is short enough to share (2-5 min); the full film is separate. Always also cut a 9:16 teaser.
- **Rhythm:** cuts land on the beat or a lyric. Alternate slow motion with real speed (never all slow-mo). Use speed ramps inside a clip to move between them.
- **Look:** one consistent grade with natural skin tones, soft highlights and a gentle film curve; optional 2.39:1 letterbox for the film, never for the 9:16 teaser.
- **Type:** names / title in Cinzel 700 or Cormorant Garamond Light Italic, tracked; date in small Inter caps; at most 2 title moments.
- **Sound:** room for vows and laughter (duck the score 8-12 dB under speech); risers only into the song change.
- **Licence:** public posting needs licensed music (`sound-and-music-design`).

## 4. Tutorial / screen recording / SaaS (new; product animation in `app-product-animation`)
- **One objective per video.** State the result in the first 5 s (show the finished thing), then the steps.
- **Rhythm:** cut all dead time (loading, typing errors, mouse wandering). A visual change at least every 10 s when the screen would sit still: zoom, callout, step number.
- **Zooms:** a precision tool, not decoration. Zoom to where the viewer must click or read, keep enough context, ease in and out (`out3`). Record at the highest resolution so zooms stay sharp.
- **Cursor:** a visible cursor with a click ring or highlight when the target is hard to find; smooth it if it jumps. Pause about 0.5 s after each big UI change.
- **Type:** Inter 500-700 labels, step pill 1-2-3, JetBrains Mono for code and shortcuts, keyboard keys as rounded chips.
- **Sound:** voice peaking -6 to -12 dB, music -25 to -30 dB, a soft click on each callout.
- **Webcam:** a corner bubble or a split at the step intros only, so the screen stays readable.

## 5. Ad / UGC (new)
- **Beats:** hook 0-3 s (the scroll-stopper decides in about 1.5 s; product or result on screen in the first 2 s), promise 3-5 s, proof / demo through the middle (problem, product in use, result), a short CTA at the end.
- **Test:** watch the first 3 s with sound off. Can you tell what it is, who it is for, and why to keep watching? Retention target: 75-80 % still watching at 3 s; under 70 % means change the first line.
- **Rhythm:** cut every silence over 0.3 s (recovers about 20 % of runtime), cuts on the music beat, the voice starts at once (no throat-clearing).
- **Look:** raw, phone-real (UGC) or clean studio (brand), never both in one ad. Captions always on: Montserrat or Poppins 800, white + black outline, hook line hand-tightened.
- **Variants:** one shoot becomes 5-15 ads by swapping hooks, first lines and CTAs; name each file by hook.
- **Rules:** no fake reviews or invented claims; on-screen prices and offers exactly as the client gives them.

## 6. Meme / gaming / fan edit (new)
- Velocity edits (speed ramps on the beat), flashes, shakes, zoom punches, glow and chromatic accents are part of the genre and allowed here only (`beginner-to-pro-edit-tips` still caps each move's size).
- Beat-map the song first; every hit gets a move; quiet parts get slow, smooth shots.
- Text: Anton / Archivo Expanded / Permanent Marker, short and loud. Copyright: a fan edit of someone else's footage is for study only (CLAUDE.md Rule 1.6); never deliver one built on footage the user does not own.

## 7. Across every form (pro rules found in all the research)
1. The first 1-3 s decide; rework the opening before dropping a clip.
2. Change something on screen whenever the information changes (framing, graphic, B-roll, sound), more often early, never randomly.
3. One special move at a time; spread the layers.
4. One grade for the whole video; one accent colour (unless a client palette says otherwise).
5. J- and L-cuts hide the edit; jump cuts remove dead air.
6. Sound on every visual event, ducked under the voice.
7. Deliver 9:16 and 16:9 from the same project when the form needs both.
8. Check on a phone before delivery.
