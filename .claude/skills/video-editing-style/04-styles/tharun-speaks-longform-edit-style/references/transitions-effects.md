# Transitions and effects

## Default: the hard cut
A-roll <-> chalkboard graphic are straight cuts (verified 2:31.4, 7:47.0 frame by frame: one frame graphic, next frame A-roll, no blend).

## Punch-in
Mid-take jump cut with ~1.2-1.3x scale (7:51.75, face jumps larger in one frame). Alternate 100% / ~120% on consecutive cuts. Combined with a slow 3-6% push over a long take.

## Flash/glow transitions (sponsor segment only)
Icon tiles on a purple glow strobe-in at 5:02.75, horizontal motion-blur streak 5:03.0-5:03.5, then settle. Use only to introduce the ad.

## Shrink-to-card
A-roll shrinks into a small framed B&W card centred on black (2:31.4-3:00) for reaction beats; scale 32-35%, hard cut in.

## Colour effects
- B&W for jokes and story beats; colour returns by cut.
- Warm grade on A-roll (see sound-and-colour.md).
- Teal accent only on graphics lines.

## Texture stack for chalkboard scenes (Suggested values)
Film grain 8-12%, vignette 35-45% darkening at corners, tiny paper texture overlay at 20%, optional soft white bloom 10% on text.

## Avoid
Whips, spins, glitch, zooms on graphics, long dissolves, lens flares, emoji.

## V2 additions (Measured 2026-11-20)
- Punch-in ratio measured from face width: 1.28x median (1.20-1.39x). Half of consecutive A-roll cuts punch in, a quarter pull back, a quarter hold size. Slow drift-in about 1-2%/s on takes over 2 s.
- Headline + jump cut + punch-in land on one frame.
- One-off transitions (each used once in 11:46): hand swipe toward the lens into the title card; one white-inverted flash frame out of it; fast scroll with motion blur on a screen recording ending on a yellow highlight box; 0.76 s of black before the sponsor; 0.4 s of TV colour bars after a bleeped joke.
- Escalation zoom gag: 5 punch-ins of 0.4-0.6 s climbing to 2.8x on a censored (pixelated) gesture.
- B&W shrink card in V2 is 65-70% of frame width (V1: 32-35%); held 1.9-3.1 s.
- White-matte montage in the hook: clips as cards on pure white, alternating with full-frame clips, 0.4-0.9 s each.
The "Avoid" list above still holds: no whips, spins, glitches or long dissolves anywhere in V2.
