# Sound and colour

## Loudness (Measured)
-10.3 LUFS integrated, LRA 2.9 LU, +0.8 dBFS peak. Very compressed voice. For new work target -12 to -10 LUFS with a limiter at -1 dBTP; check true peak.

## Music/SFX (Measured on V2 with the stereo side channel, 2026-11-20)
- Music runs in BLOCKS, not as a continuous bed: on for the hook, title card and energetic items; fully off for stories and around jokes (V2: off 1:10-2:50, 3:20-3:40, 6:10-6:30). Plan music on/off per section in the PLAN, with reasons.
- When on, the stereo music layer is 15-22 dB under the voice (voice -13.4 dBFS RMS) and bass-heavy (half its energy under 120 Hz), leaving the voice band clear. Loudest in the first 20 s.
- Sound effects mark only big graphics: 30% of headline pops and 26% of cuts into graphic scenes carry a stereo hit/whoosh (chance 12%). Plain cuts and caption pops get none. A bleep + 0.4 s colour bars is used once as a gag.
- Pauses of 1 s+ in the voice are always covered by a graphic beat with music or an effect, never by silent A-roll.
- V2 master: -10.5 LUFS, LRA 3.2 LU, peak +1.6 dBFS (clipping in the source; keep new work at -1 dBTP).
- Source from Mixkit / Freesound / Pixabay (user decision 2026-11-14).

## Colour
- A-roll: warm, low-key practicals, deep orange-brown shadows, olive/green clothing preserved; skin slightly warm.
- Graphics: near-monochrome. Red for negatives, teal for connectors, glass highlights white.
- Ad scenes: white background with purple/blue gradients.

## Colour, V2 additions (Observed)
- Same warm low-key A-roll as V1: one practical floor lamp, orange-brown walls, deep black left edge, olive-brown polo; skin warm, not saturated.
- Dark scenes are charcoal radial gradients (#161616 edge to #3a3a3a centre) with a faint grid and grain, instead of V1's chalkboard paper.
- Accent is orange (#ff7a1a to #ffc233 gradient with a red-orange glow) on the title and the counter tab. Red glowing dots = problems, yellow dots = options, green = good months. White type.
- Hook B-roll is B&W or very low-key with fine horizontal scan lines; the white-matte montage is the only bright moment before the sponsor read.
