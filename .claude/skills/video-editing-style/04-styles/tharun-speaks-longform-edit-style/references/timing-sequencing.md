# Timing and sequencing

## Shot length targets (Measured from 209 shots)
- Median 2.4 s. Keep 18% of shots under 1 s (quick punch/B-roll), 58% at 1-3 s, 24-33% at 3-6 s. Allow one 8-16 s hold when the speaker tells a story.

## Pattern per tip (about 45-70 s)
1. Title scene (chalkboard, 2-4 s): brain with the tip's node + plate, or "SECRET N".
2. A-roll setup 6-12 s with captions on the key nouns.
3. Explainer graphic 3-8 s (diagram, card, example).
4. A-roll punch-in 3-6 s with behind-head headline on the key line.
5. Repeat 2-4 once or twice for examples.
6. Close with a 1-2 s reaction/B&W joke if the script has one, then "Let's go to the next one" and the next title.

## Graphic timing
- Graphic appears 0.2-0.5 s BEFORE the word is finished being said and holds until the idea is done (2-6 s).
- Headline holds 1.5-3 s.
- Caption block: 3-8 s long, then removed.

## Sections
- Hook: 55 s, high density (a visual every 2-3 s).
- Ad read: bright scene set, 20-30 s, one scene per benefit.
- Activity: ask to comment; show checklist graphic.
- End: A-roll only, brush quote caption, calm.

## V2 numbers (Measured 2026-11-20)
- 238 shots in 706.7 s = 20.2/min; median 2.06 s, mean 2.97 s, p10 0.67 s, p90 6.0 s; 20% under 1 s, 48% 1-3 s, 32% 3 s+.
- Cuts per minute: 34 in the hook minute, 20-25 through the lessons, 11 in the notepad minute, 4 in the last minute.
- Item lengths: 84, 36, 40, 38, 53, (sponsor 40), 3, 16, 26, 56, 71, 7, 2, 53, 36 s, then a 90 s epilogue and a 6 s end card.
- Full-screen graphic holds: counter 1.2-2.9 s, title 2.4 s, quote 2.2-2.9 s, metaphor scene 3.5-8.2 s, chart up to 8.8 s, notepad 12.8 s.
- Longest plain A-roll take inside the lessons: 14.4 s. In the epilogue: 30.6 s.

## Item template v2 (36-56 s)
1. Counter, number only (1.2-2.8 s).
2. A-roll with lesson tag; first sentence as a behind-head caption.
3. Side graphics on the nouns (icons, cards, list) while the speaker stays on screen.
4. One full-screen metaphor scene or chart (3-8 s).
5. A-roll punch line with the headline sandwich on a punch-in.
6. Optional: counter again with the lesson's name typed on it, or a 1-2 s gag.
Vary the list with 2-3 very short items (a silent quote card, a one-line joke).

## Video skeleton v2
Cold open with proof and an open loop (16 s) → premise over fast B-roll (15 s) → promise (14 s) → title card (2.4 s) → items 1-5 → sponsor (40 s, about 42%) → items 6-14 → epilogue paying off the open loop (90 s) → text end card (6 s).
