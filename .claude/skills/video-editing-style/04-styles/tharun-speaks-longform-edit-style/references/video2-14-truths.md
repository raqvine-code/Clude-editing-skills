# Video 2 study: "14 Brutally Honest Truths I wish I knew at 18"

Studied 2026-11-20. Whole video, 706.7 s, 3840x1920 (2:1), 25 fps, 14-lesson list video with a sponsor read and an epilogue.
Reference only: never reuse the creator's face, footage, script or sponsor (Odoo) branding.

## How it was studied (coverage)
- Every shot viewed once (238 mid-shot frames on 6 sheets), 9 frame-by-frame strips (8-12.5 fps) on the key graphics, 12 full-quality stills for type and texture.
- All on-screen text read by OCR at 5 fps (3,533 frames, 847 text events).
- Face box tracked at 5 fps (2,708 frames) for framing size, punch-in ratio and push-in.
- Audio: EBU R128 loudness, word-level transcript (2,316 words), stereo side channel (L-R) to separate music/SFX from the centred voice.

## Sound map (Measured)
- Voice sits at -13.4 dBFS RMS; the stereo-only layer (music + stereo SFX) averages -30 dBFS, so 15-22 dB under the voice when it plays. Half of that layer's energy is below 120 Hz: the bed is bassy and stays out of the voice band.
- Music runs in blocks, not as a constant bed (stereo level per section): ON 0:00-1:10 (loudest in the first 20 s, dips under the premise, back for the title) · OFF 1:10-2:50 (100 s, the story plays dry) · ON 2:50-3:20 · OFF 3:20-3:40 · ON 3:40-5:00 · low 5:00-5:30 (sponsor) · ON 5:30-6:10 · OFF 6:10-6:30 (joke lands dry) · ON 6:30-8:30 · low 8:30-10:10 · gentle rise through the epilogue.
- Sound effects: a stereo burst lands within 0.3 s of 30% of headline moments and 26% of cuts into dark graphic scenes (chance level 12%). Plain cuts 11% (chance 9%) and caption pops 10% (chance 12%) get nothing. So: SFX on roughly a quarter to a third of the big graphic entries only.
- Every pause of 1 s or more is filled by a silent graphic beat (title card, quote cards, gag, counter). There is no dead air on A-roll.

## Limits
- Sound effects were detected as energy bursts in the stereo layer, not identified by type; mono effects would be missed.
- Font names are matches by eye.
- The push-in figure comes from a face box and is noisy.
- Transcript stops at 10:55 (the last 50 s of the epilogue were not transcribed).
- OCR cannot read headlines hidden by the head; the headline list was built from the shot frames.
