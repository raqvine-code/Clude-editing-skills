# Typography and captions

Four roles, one font each. Free matches given; all are Observed fits, not identifications.

| Role | Look | Free match | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caption/label sans | Clean grotesque, semibold, white, soft shadow, lower-centre | Inter or Inter Tight 600-700 | Keyword captions, UI labels, ad scene text |
| Giant headline | Very condensed, heavy, pure white, behind subject | Anton or Bebas Neue (squeeze 80-85%) | "10/10", "DEADLINE SUPERCHARGE", "HUMBLE FLEX", "VIDEO EDITING" |
| Plate caps | Condensed caps, embossed on grey rounded button-plate, big numeral | Bebas Neue/Oswald on #6b6b6b plate with inner bevel | "01 BREAK IT DOWN", "FINAL OUTPUT", "FAKE PASSION" |
| Brush | Hand-drawn chalk/marker caps, uneven baseline | Permanent Marker / Rock Salt / "Caveat Brush" | Quote captions, "MISTAKE" stack, "MOST IMPORTANT?" |

## Caption rules
1. Captions run in blocks of a few seconds, not through the whole video; quiet stretches have none.
2. Lower-centre, about 78-84% of frame height (measured by eye 4:55-5:03). Never over the face.
3. 1-3 words per swap. Swap on the spoken word. A word sits 0.25-0.9 s.
4. Emphasis is by size, not colour: key words ("connect", "hire") are 1.5-2x the filler words ("a", "we used").
5. White fill, no outline, soft drop shadow ~30% opacity; on white cards use near-black.
6. Brush captions: two lines, centred, uppercase, second line dimmed to 40% when it is a follow-on.
7. Tiny labels inside graphics (chat bubbles, tiles, pills) use the sans in 20-30 px at 4K.

## Headline rules
- Max 2 words per line, 2 lines max; stack or side-by-side.
- Width 60-90% of frame; sits behind head and shoulders (matte), letters partly hidden by the subject is intended.
- Pure white (#FFFFFF), slight motion blur on entry frames only; no outline.
- Appears exactly on the keyword, leaves with the shot.

## Sizes for a 3840x1920 canvas (Suggested, tune by eye)
- Caption: 150-190 px filler, 300 px key word.
- Headline: cap height 700-900 px.
- Plate caps: 90-140 px.

## V2 caption system (Measured 2026-11-20, see video2-14-truths.md)
V1 used lower-centre keyword pops. V2 uses three caption types; pick by section:
| Type | Where | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Two-size hook caption | Hook only, lower-centre over B-roll and A-roll | Lead-in line box 5% of frame height over a keyword 16-21%; one chunk per clip, 0.7-1.4 s |
| Behind-head sentence caption | Lesson A-roll | One key sentence, box 4.9% of frame height (about 94 px at 1920 high, 53 px at 1080), at eye level (30-60% of frame height), running behind the head; up to 2 lines; words appear with the voice; holds 3-4.5 s |
| Dash list | Left of the speaker | Rows start with "-"; current row white, others grey 50% |
- Placement count over 386 caption events: 56% left of the head, 26% centre, 17% right. Almost nothing in the lower third outside the hook.
- Timing: text lands 0-0.3 s after the word begins (median +0.19 s). Median event: 2 words, 0.8 s.
- Coverage: 17% of spoken words (hook 35%). Caption the thesis sentence of each item, not everything.
- Tracking is tight (about -3%), sentence case or all lowercase, white with soft shadow, no outline, no colour highlight.

## Headline sandwich (V2, Measured)
Lead-in (small sans, top-left, aligned to the headline's left edge) + giant headline behind the head (box 41-57% of frame height, 30-39% for two words) + continuation (small sans, bottom-right, IN FRONT of the chest). The headline lands on the same frame as a jump cut with a punch-in. Examples: "but with a / CAUTION", "If you can't do it / COLLEGE / while you're in", "The best way to learn a / CREATIVE SKILL / is to".
A three-word stack ("MOTIVATION / CONSISTENCY / & INTELLECT") may sit to the right of the head instead, about 17% of frame height per line.

## Lesson tag (V2)
"LESSON : n" in white marker handwriting inside a hand-drawn oval, top-left at x 3-8%, y 3-7%, about 4% of frame height. On screen for the whole lesson, including over graphics.
