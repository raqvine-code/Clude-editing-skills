## Principles
1. One idea per scene. Max 3 new elements per second.
2. Entrances are short (3-12 frames). Ease out, no bounce beyond slight overshoot on plates.
3. Elements are connected with thin lines; lines draw after the nodes.
4. Everything is flat-lit; depth comes from grain, vignette and glass highlights, not 3D camera moves.
5. Only the brain, orbit and bead scenes drift; text never rotates.
6. Cut away within 0.3 s after the narration finishes the idea.

## V2 additions (2026-11-20)
The full V2 catalogue with measured timings is in `video2-14-truths.md` ("Graphics catalogue"): folder counter, title card, lesson tag, headline sandwich, behind-head captions, dash lists, side labels, glass side icons/clocks/thumbnails, mannequin metaphor scenes, calendar grids, charts, notepad, quote cards, sponsor counters, white-matte montage.
New principles confirmed by V2:
7. Prefer a graphic BESIDE the speaker (icons, cards, lists at head height) for a 2-6 s idea; cut away to a full-screen scene only for a metaphor, chart or document.
8. Side graphics are parented to the frame: they survive jump cuts and scale with the punch-in.
9. A metaphor scene has one symbol, a few dots, two labels and exactly one meaningful movement.
10. Text types on with the voice, one word per 0.2-0.25 s; rows of a list light one at a time.
11. The chapter counter enters by hard cut already moving: slide + scale 4-6 frames, tab 5 frames, name typed in 0.5 s, hold 1 s.
