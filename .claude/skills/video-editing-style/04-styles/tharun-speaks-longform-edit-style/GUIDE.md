# Tharun Speaks - long-form edit style

Reverse-engineered from TWO reference videos, both 3840x1920 (2:1), 25 fps, numbered list videos, NOT reels:
- **V1** "How to Learn So Fast People Think You're Naturally Gifted", first 600 s, 9 tips (studied 2026-11-15).
- **V2** "14 Brutally Honest Truths I wish I knew at 18", whole video 707 s, 14 lessons (studied 2026-11-20: every shot viewed, all on-screen text read, framing and stereo audio measured). Full notes: `references/video2-14-truths.md`.

A sibling skill, `tharun-speaks-edit-style`, covers the vertical 9:16 reel version. Use this skill to apply the style to the editor's OWN footage and script. Never reuse the reference creator's face, footage, script or sponsor branding.

## Evidence labels (read every rule with its label)
- **Measured**: read from frames/audio/cut detection. Trust.
- **Observed**: seen consistently in frames and strips, no number.
- **Suggested**: standard practice that fits the style; not confirmed in the video.
- **Both**: found in V1 and V2, so it is the creator's habit, not one video's choice. Copy these first.

Limits: two videos studied; fonts are closest-free matches; sound effects were detected as bursts, not identified by type.

## A. Constants (Both videos)
1. **Cut rhythm.** 20 cuts/min (V1 20.9, V2 20.2). Median shot 2.0-2.4 s, mean 2.9-3.0 s, p90 5.6-6.0 s. V2 mix: 20% under 1 s, 48% 1-3 s, 32% 3 s+. The hook is the fastest minute (34 cuts), the ending the slowest (4). (Measured, Both)
2. **Two worlds, hard-cut between them.** Warm A-roll (centred speaker, desk room, practical lamp, shallow depth) against dark graphic scenes. In V2 the speaker is on screen 75% of the runtime; full-screen graphics and B-roll take 25%. Straight cuts; no dissolves. (Measured V2, Observed Both)
3. **Punch-in on jump cuts.** Face stays dead centre (x 0.50, y 0.43). A jump cut re-frames by 1.28x on average (middle half 1.20-1.39x). Of consecutive A-roll cuts, half punch in, a quarter pull back, a quarter keep the size. Most takes of 2 s+ also drift in slowly (about 1-2%/s). (Measured V2, matches V1 by eye)
4. **Giant headline behind the head.** 1-2 condensed white caps words, 40-55% of frame height (30-39% when two words), between background and speaker (person matte). It arrives fully formed on the SAME frame as a jump cut + punch-in, on the lesson's thesis word, and leaves with the shot after 1-4 s. About one per lesson (V2: 15 in 11:46). (Measured, Both)
5. **One chapter-counter device per video, reused at every item.** V1: wireframe brain with numbered nodes. V2: row of grey folder tiles, active tile with an orange glowing tab. 1.2-2.9 s each, hard cut in and out. Design one for each new video; never copy these two. (Observed, Both)
6. **Selective captions, never subtitles.** Only 17% of spoken words reach the screen (hook 35%). Text appears 0-0.3 s after the word starts, never before. Emphasis by size, not colour. (Measured V2, Observed Both)
7. **Speech is cut airtight.** 213 words/min, median gap between words 0 s; V2 has only 9 pauses of 1 s+ and each one is filled by a silent graphic beat (title card, quote card, counter, gag). No dead air on A-roll. (Measured V2)
8. **Loud master.** -10.3 / -10.5 LUFS, LRA about 3 LU, peaks above 0 dBFS in the source. For new work: -12 to -10 LUFS with a limiter at -1 dBTP (do not copy the clipping). (Measured, Both)
9. **Sponsor read starts at 4:40-5:00** (42% of runtime in V2; V1's full length was not supplied), 40-45 s, its own bright look, a thin white progress line, one scene per benefit, QR beside the speaker. (Observed, Both)
10. **Near-monochrome graphics + one accent + red for negatives.** V1 accent teal/green lines; V2 orange. Grain and vignette on every dark scene. (Observed, Both)
11. **B&W for story and joke beats**, full-frame or shrunk into a card on black, colour returns by cut. (Observed, Both)
12. **Speaker is never away for long.** Full-screen graphics run 2-8 s (one 12.8 s notepad hold in V2); the speaker returns on every punch line. (Measured V2)
13. **A visual for every beat of meaning.** Each item opens on the counter, and each noun, number or example gets its own 2-6 s visual; on-screen text of some kind is present 73% of the time in V2 (hook 91%, ending 45%). (Measured V2, Observed Both)
14. **Graphics enter simply and read in under a second.** Pop, fade or scale in 3-12 frames, text types on with the voice, lines draw after their nodes. No wipes, spins or glitches. (Measured on strips, Both)
15. **Four type roles, one font each**: tight sans for captions and labels, heavy condensed caps for headlines, marker handwriting for tags and brush lines, light sans for quotes (see `references/typography-captions.md`). (Observed, Both)
16. **Calm ending.** A-roll only, long takes (V2: 15-31 s), text density drops, a plain text end card. (Observed, Both)

## B. Devices that change per video (choose ONE set, do not mix)
| Slot | V1 | V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter counter | Wireframe brain, lit node | Folder tiles, orange tab; number-only opens a lesson, number + typed name closes it |
| Dark world | Chalkboard paper, heavy grain | Charcoal radial gradient with faint grid |
| Explainer language | Plates, glass beads, glass cards, X-ray head | Grey clay mannequin + glowing dots, calendars, charts, notepad |
| Captions in the body | Lower-centre keyword pops | Behind-head sentence captions at eye level |
| Persistent tag | none | Hand-drawn "LESSON : n" oval, top-left |
| Accent | Teal/green lines | Orange (title, tab) |

## C. Moves learned from V2
17. **Headline sandwich.** Small lead-in line top-left → giant headline behind the head → small continuation bottom-right in front of the chest, added one word per 0.2-0.25 s with the voice. Reads as one sentence: "Don't try to solve the / PROBLEM / learn to walk away from it." (Measured)
18. **Behind-head sentence caption.** One key sentence in small tight sans (box 4.9% of frame height) at eye level, running behind the head so the head hides the middle; words appear as spoken; the sentence holds 3-4.5 s until the next one cuts in. 56% of caption text sits left of the head, 17% right, 26% centre; almost none in the lower third. (Measured)
19. **Graphics beside the speaker.** Glass icons, clock cards, own thumbnails and short lists pop in next to the head on the spoken word, stay through punch-in cuts and scale with the frame. Use these before cutting away to a full-screen graphic. (Observed)
20. **Metaphor scene.** One symbol for "you", coloured glowing dots for problems or options, two tiny labels, and ONE movement that acts out the lesson (walk out of the ring = walk away from the problem). 3.5-8 s. (Observed)
21. **Lists light one row at a time**: current row white, the rest grey 50%. (Observed)
22. **Silent quote card as a list item.** A 2-3 s quote on dark with no speech counts as a lesson. Three very short items (2 s, 3 s, 7 s) break the rhythm between 36-84 s lessons. (Measured)
23. **Counter closes the loop.** Some lessons end with the counter tile showing the lesson's name typed on it, then the next number. (Observed)
24. **Music in blocks.** Music is ON for the hook, title and energetic lessons, and fully OFF for long dry stretches (100 s during the first story, 20 s around a joke); it is not a constant bed. When on, the stereo layer sits 15-22 dB under the voice and is bass-heavy. (Measured)
25. **Sound effects only on big graphics.** About a quarter to a third of headline pops and cuts into graphic scenes carry a stereo hit or whoosh. Plain cuts and caption pops are silent. (Measured)
26. **Gags are edited, not just said**: pixelation with 5 escalating punch-ins (0.4-0.6 s each, up to 2.8x), meme face cut-out, 0.4 s of TV colour bars with a bleep. One or two per video. (Observed)
27. **Hook recipe (0-47 s).** Greeting + name headline → proof on screen (own channel page, the number highlighted) → open loop ("more at the end") → premise over fast B-roll with two-size captions (clips 0.4-1.4 s) → "this may contradict what others teach" → "if you're stuck… let's get started" → title card inside the pause. The open loop is paid off in the epilogue. (Measured)
28. **Title card**: enters after a hand swipe toward the lens, number first, then the rest, sub-line typed, 2.4 s total, exits on one white flash frame. The only flash in the video. (Measured)
29. **Lesson order**: the longest story first (84 s), most lessons 36-56 s, the second-longest late (71 s), sponsor after lesson 5 of 14. (Measured)

## Workflow in one page
1. Transcribe; number the items; mark for each item the thesis word (headline), 1-2 key sentences (captions), and each noun or example worth a visual.
2. Rough-cut the A-roll airtight (no gap over 0.3 s unless a graphic will fill it). Jump cut every 2-4 s; alternate sizes with a 1.2-1.4x punch-in; add a slow push on takes over 2 s.
3. Design this video's device set (section B): counter, dark-world texture, explainer language, caption system, accent. Build each as a template once.
4. Hook by the recipe in rule 27, then the title card.
5. Per item: counter (number) → A-roll with tag and sentence captions → side graphics on nouns → one full-screen metaphor or chart (3-8 s) → A-roll punch line with the headline sandwich → optional counter (name).
6. Place 1-2 edited gags and 1-2 silent quote items where the script allows.
7. Sound: voice forward; music in blocks (on for hook, title, energetic items; off for stories and jokes); hits only on headlines and graphic-scene entries; master -12 to -10 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
8. Warm grade on A-roll; grain + vignette on dark scenes; render a 60 s sample; check; render.
9. End with a calm A-roll epilogue and a plain text end card.
