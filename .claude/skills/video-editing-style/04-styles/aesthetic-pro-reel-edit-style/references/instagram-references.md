## Numbers across the 11 references
- Cuts or scene changes: one per **1.9-2.5 s** on podcast / talking-head clips (I3, I4, I7). The calm portfolio pieces (I1, I2) hold 5-14 s on one shot but change the TEXT every 1 s.
- Every reference uses at least two type weights per line, usually a **sans + an italic serif or script**. None uses one font only.
- The keyword is ONE colour per video (red I1, olive I3, white-bold I4, mustard I5 and I8, orange I6, lime I11).
- Designed inserts (paper scene, slam card, ribbon type, checklist card, HUD, ring) appear about **once every 5-8 s** in the "advanced" pieces (I3, I5, I9, I11).
- Grades: dark and warm (I1, I5, I6, I8) or desaturated natural (I4, I7). None is bright and saturated.

## Web notes (2026, mostly tool-vendor blogs, so read them as direction only)
- Text behind a person is built with a mask around the subject; moving text gets motion blur so it does not look "harshly digital"; distant text can be softer and fainter for depth ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/how-to-put-text-behind-a-person-in-capcut), [CapCut depth-of-field text](https://www.capcut.com/create/3d-typography-hierarchy-without-3d-software), [Filmora](https://filmora.wondershare.com/basic-video-editing/perspective-text.html)).
- High-production accounts are also moving to cinematic frames, slow zooms, a restrained grade and minimal text ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/editing-aesthetics-dominating-short-form-2026)): the "advanced" look is layered, not loud.
- Podcast clips: add B-roll, screenshots or charts only where they clarify; J/L cuts for invisible transitions; rework the first 3 s before dropping a clip ([CapCut podcast clips](https://www.capcut.com/create/podcast-clips-for-social-media-long-form-editing), [Quso](https://quso.ai/blog/how-to-edit-podcast-shorts-like-a-pro.html), [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/how-to-clip-a-podcast)).
