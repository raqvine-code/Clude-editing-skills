# Second reel analysis (reel B)

Reel B is a second reference reel by the same creator: 88.6 s, 1080x1920, 25 fps, 2,216 frames, 31 shots. It was studied the same way as reel A: cut detection on every frame, half-second contact sheets of the whole reel, frame-by-frame strips around the hook and every transition, and pixel measurements.

Where this file disagrees with the other reference files, both are true: the other files describe reel A. Treat points the two reels share as the style, and points where they differ as choices to make per video.

The audio was analysed as a signal, never heard.

## What is the same in both reels

- One or two words per caption, white, bold, same font, soft shadow, hard pop on the spoken word.
- Emphasis stacks: small lead-in over a big keyword.
- Giant condensed type behind the speaker's head in the hook and proof sections.
- Punch-in jump cuts on the same take: 124%, 129%, 133% and 143% measured in reel B; returns at 75%.
- A list of five steps, each opened by the same repeating full-screen step scene.
- Negative flash and white flash transitions, 1 to 2 frames.
- Black captions on white card scenes, and a white QR card for the call to action.
- A loud, flat, voice-first mix: -9.5 LUFS, 1.6 LU range.
- Props on a gridded desk that stand for the idea of the video.

## New devices in reel B

### Counting numbers

A number runs up to its final value, then holds.

| Counter | Where | Run | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rupee amount | floating over a glowing prop | about 20 frames | neon green #D4FD2D, bold caption font, with glow |
| Percentage | behind the head, top of frame | about 30 frames | condensed caps, white-to-yellow gradient, yellow #FDE944 |
| "500+" | on the chest, behind the caption | about 20 frames | condensed caps, neon lime #E3FE00, small white word under it |

The count slows as it nears the end: big jumps first, small steps last.

### Glowing props

The two glasses on the desk are relit in the edit: a green glow and a red glow, each with a floating value label in the matching colour. The hook cuts to a macro of each prop in turn. The props stay as a visual anchor when the speaker returns.

### Spotlight step scene

Replaces the coin scene of reel A.

- Deep navy room (#11315C at the centre, near-black at the edges) with a warm spotlight cone from above.
- A pile of papers on a desk. For each step, a paper flies up out of the pile, turns to face the camera and shows "STEP n" and the step title in condensed caps.
- Papers from earlier steps stay standing in front of the pile, small.
- The scene opens with 2 to 3 frames of vertical motion blur.
- Length 1.8 to 2.5 s. Used five times.

### Glass cards on dark navy

One translucent, softly lit UI card per idea, centred, on a near-black navy background with a faint colour glow behind the card. Cards rise into place one after another. Captions sit above the card at about 28% of frame height. A cursor may click a button on the card.

### Flow diagram with camera travel

Grey background (#7C7C77 centre, darker at the corners), white line icons in thin circles, translucent panels, thin white connector lines. The scene starts on a wide view of the whole diagram, then pushes in and travels along the connectors from stage to stage, with a short label at each stage. 7.0 s with no cut. It holds attention because the camera never stops.

### Recap card

A light card (#F0EDEB) on navy with a small heading. The lines of the summary type on one by one in the order they are spoken, with indented sub-points. Then the camera moves down to a slider with a value tag. 5.2 s.

### Icon strip

A horizontal band of round icons slides across the chest under the caption, with the current one highlighted. It previews the steps.

### Tinted and list captions

- A key word or phrase fades in tinted orange (#D78D5C) or blue, then settles to white. About 8 frames.
- A list is spoken as a left-aligned stack near the left edge (x 60 to 400), one line added per item, with a thin blue streak running down beside it.
- A long sentence may split into a left block and a right block at the same height.

### Blur-zoom transition

Three to four frames of heavy directional or zoom blur across a cut, going into or out of a graphic scene or a tight punch-in. Text on screen blurs with the picture.
