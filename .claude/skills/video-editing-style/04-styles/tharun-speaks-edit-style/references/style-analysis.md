# Style analysis: the evidence

This file records what was studied, how each number was measured, and the full shot log of the reference reel. Use it when the editor asks "why" or "where does that number come from", and when you need a worked example of a scene.

## What was studied

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reference reel | 84.57 s, 1080x1920, 25 fps, 2,111 frames, H.264 video, stereo AAC audio |
| Screenshots | 19 stills at 1080x1920. 13 are frames from the reference reel. 6 are from a second reel by the same creator that was not supplied as video |
| Method | Every frame compared with the next to find cuts. Contact sheets viewed at half-second steps for the whole reel, then frame by frame around every transition and graphic. Captions tracked on all 2,111 frames. Pixel measurements taken at full resolution |

## How each number was measured

| Number | Method | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 36 shots, cut times | Frame-difference spikes, each checked by eye. Jump cuts inside a take confirmed from before and after frames | High |
| Caption beat durations | The white caption shape tracked on every frame; a new beat is counted when the shape changes. 173 beats over the A-roll and card scenes | High |
| Caption size and position | Letter bounding boxes on 11 frames. Lowercase x-height is 64 px; single-line captions centre at y 1304 | High |
| Caption font fit | The same words rendered in candidate fonts and compared by width. Inter Tight at weight 600-700, 116-118 px, tracking -2% to -3% fits five test captions within 1-2% | Medium: a close match, not an identification |
| Display font fit | Line width to cap height is 4.76 and stem to cap height is 0.145 in the reel. Seven free condensed fonts tested; all are 15-28% wider at normal width | Medium |
| No caption animation | Width, height and pixel area of 15 captions on their first five frames. All are at final size on frame one | High |
| Punch-in sizes | Feature matching between the frames either side of a jump cut: 1.27x, 1.39x, 1.43x in; 0.77x, 0.79x out | High for 1.27 and 1.39, medium for the others |
| Push-in rates | Feature matching between the first and last frames of a take | Medium. Only takes with many matched points are quoted |
| Colours | Median pixel values sampled inside each element | High, within video compression error |
| Loudness | EBU R128 measurement of the whole mix | High |
| Bass bed and tempo | Energy below 90 Hz in the pauses between words; spacing of low-frequency hits | High for presence, medium for exact tempo |
| Cuts versus beat | Distance from each cut to the nearest kick, compared with chance | High: no relationship |
| Sound effects | Not measurable. Onset detection could not separate effects from speech | Unknown |

## Things that could not be confirmed

- Which sound effects are used, and whether there is a quiet melodic bed between 12 s and 52 s.
- The original font files.
- Whether timing numbers hold across the creator's other videos. Only one reel was available as video.
- Details inside shots 22 and 27 to 35 beyond captions and framing. They were checked at half-second steps, not frame by frame.

## What the second reel's stills add

The six stills from the second reel show the same grammar with different colours:

- the same caption font, white with soft shadow, hard-edged, one or two words;
- the same stack: a small lead-in over a giant condensed keyword behind or over the speaker;
- captions centred near 50% of frame height, because the speaker is framed tighter;
- graphic scenes on dark navy and grey gradients with glass-style cards, thin white line icons, a spotlight illustration and a slider graphic, instead of the purple and lime of the reference reel.

Conclusion: the type system, layout zones and pacing are the style. The colour family and the illustration set change per video.
