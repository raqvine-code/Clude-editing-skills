# Transitions and effects

The style uses very few transitions and very few effects, and uses them the same way every time. Restraint is part of the look.

Frames are at 25 fps. Labels: Measured, Approx, Suggested.

## Transition count in the reference reel

| Transition | Uses | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | 30 | 86% |
| Negative flash | 3 | 9% |
| White flash | 2 | 6% |
| Dissolve, wipe, slide, zoom blur, glitch, whip pan | 0 | 0% |

## Hard cut

The default. The picture changes on the frame a new phrase starts. Captions carry straight across the cut: the beat that is on screen changes with the word, not with the picture.

Three kinds of hard cut:

| Kind | What changes | Example in the reel |
|---|---|---|
| Jump cut in the same take | only the framing size | 9.20, 10.68, 15.68, 20.28, 74.88 s |
| Cut to a graphic scene | everything | 11.60, 21.64, 33.36, 36.08 s |
| Cut back to the speaker | everything | 24.88, 35.36, 38.00 s |

## Negative flash

Two frames between two shots.

| Frame | Picture |
|---|---|
| 1 | the last frame of the outgoing shot, inverted and with all colour removed |
| 2 | black |
| 3 | the first frame of the new shot |

Details, all Measured:

- The effect sits under the caption layer. On frame 1 the caption is still white with its shadow. On frame 2 there is no caption.
- Overlays in the shot (icon badges, headline text) are inverted along with the footage.
- No sound gap is needed; speech runs through it.

Where it is used: 17.60 s (mid-sentence, between the set-up and the punchline), 44.20 s (speaker to step 3), 64.68 s (end of the list to the "mistake" section).

Use it 2 to 4 times per video. Never twice within 10 seconds.

## White flash

Two frames.

| Frame | Picture |
|---|---|
| 1 | the outgoing shot, blown out to near white |
| 2 | pure white; the next caption beat may already be on it |
| 3 | the new shot |

Where it is used: 14.32 s, leaving the paper reveal scene; 33.44 s, two frames into the first step-coin scene, as a single white frame that "switches on" the scene.

Use it once or twice per video, on the way into or out of a bright graphic scene.

## Punch-in jump cut

The same take, cut at a pause, with the second piece scaled up.

| Property | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Punch-in sizes | 127%, 139%, 143% | Measured |
| Return cut | back out by the same amount (0.77x, 0.79x) | Measured |
| Anchor | the face, about x 50%, y 36% of the frame | Measured |
| Animation | none; the size changes on the cut | Measured |
| Good moments | the punchline phrase, a stack, a direct address to the viewer | Measured |

Pattern in the reel: wide for the set-up, punched in for the payoff line, back out for the next thought. A punched-in piece usually carries an emphasis stack.

Shoot or export the A-roll with spare resolution so a 143% crop stays sharp. Suggested: 4K source for a 1080-wide edit.

## Slow push-in

| Property | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | 1.2 to 3.6% per second | Measured |
| Curve | linear, from the first frame of the take to the last | Approx |
| Used on | takes longer than about 2.5 s | Measured |
| Not used on | most takes under 1.5 s | Measured |

The hook is the exception: 100% to 116% in 1.7 s with an ease-out, then a hold.

## Snap zoom on stills

On a photo, a jump to a tighter crop with one frame of motion blur. Measured once, at 4.72 s. It behaves like a punch-in for still images.

## Looks

### A-roll grade

| Property | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Key light | warm; skin and shirt read orange-brown (about #C4754D) | Measured |
| Background | dark olive green (about #514818) with a green practical light | Measured |
| Vignette | strong; corners near black (about #190D08) | Measured |
| Lens | wide and close, slightly above the desk; strong perspective on hands and props | Approx |
| Depth | background soft, speaker sharp | Approx |
| Framing | eyes at about 32-38% of height; desk and props fill the lower third | Approx |
| Set | a black cutting mat with a white grid; small physical props that match the topic | Measured |

The warm skin against the green room is the colour contrast of the footage. Keep the room darker than the face.

### Third-party photos

Black and white, slightly darkened, with only the main person in colour. A label and pointer line name them. See the photo insert recipe.

### Text behind the subject

Slight blur plus a soft glow on the text, and a clean cut-out of the speaker in front. A rough matte edge ruins it: check hair and fingers frame by frame.

### Paper scenes

Paper grain over a warm off-white, with a vignette strong enough that the corners go grey-brown. Flat graphics on top, no shadows except under cut-outs.

### White card scenes

Almost pure white with a faint grid that fades at the edges. One object, one drop shadow.

### Coin scene

Near-black with two coloured glows and neon outlines. The only scene with a 3D-style object.

## Effects that are absent

Do not add these. None appears in the reference reel:

- animated captions: bounce, typewriter, karaoke colour fill, per-letter wiggle;
- coloured or highlighted caption words, emoji in captions;
- caption boxes or strokes;
- dissolves, cross-zooms, glitch, RGB split, shake, light leaks, film burns;
- progress bars, subscribe animations, watermark logos;
- speed ramps.

If the editor asks for one of these, say it is outside the style and let them decide.
