# Sound design

Read this first: the reference reel's audio was analysed as a signal. It was not listened to. Loudness, frequency bands, bass presence and kick spacing are measured facts. Which sound effects are used, how the music sounds and how the voice is processed are not known.

Everything under "Measured" can be trusted. Everything under "Suggested" is standard practice for this kind of reel and fits the picture edit, but it was not confirmed. Tell the editor which is which, and ask them to check the original by ear.

## Measured

### Loudness

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Integrated loudness | -9.4 LUFS |
| Loudness range | 2.4 LU |
| True peak | +2.2 dBFS |
| Channels | stereo, 44.1 kHz |

What this means:

- The mix is very loud and very flat. Speech sits at nearly the same level from the first word to the last.
- The peaks go over full scale, so the master is clipping or limited hard.
- There are no quiet passages and no music-only moments.

### Where the energy is

| Band | Share of total energy |
|---|---|
| 20-150 Hz (sub and bass) | 13% |
| 150-400 Hz (voice body) | 48% |
| 400-3,000 Hz (voice clarity) | 34% |
| above 6,000 Hz (air, hiss, bright effects) | 4% |

The voice dominates. The mix is warm and mid-heavy, not bright.

### Music bed, by section

Found from the sound level in the gaps between words.

| Time | Bass under the voice | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0-3.4 s | none | The hook is voice only, or voice with a very light bed |
| 3.4-12.0 s | strong, steady | A bass-heavy bed starts exactly on the first cut and runs under the proof section |
| 12.0-15.0 s | fading | Bed drops away through the reveal scene |
| 15.0-24.0 s | none; true silence between words at 18-21 s | No music at all under the stakes passage |
| 24.0-52.5 s | very little | No bass bed. A light bed without bass may be present; not confirmed |
| 52.5-81.5 s | strong, with a regular kick | A beat-driven track starts on the cut to step 4 and runs to the end |
| 81.5-84.4 s | fading | Music drops for the end CTA; the audio fades in the last second |

### Tempo

In the final section the low-frequency hits are 0.465 s apart, which is about 129 beats per minute.

### Cuts and the beat

Thirteen cuts fall inside the beat-driven section. Their distance to the nearest kick averages 0.117 s. Chance alone gives 0.116 s. The cuts are not placed on the beat. They follow the voice.

### Low hits that line up with pictures

| Time | Picture event |
|---|---|
| 4.15 s | name label lands on the photo |
| 6.2 s | article card rises behind the head |
| 50.0 s | cut to the mid-roll CTA card |

These suggest a low impact or boom on a few big reveals. Three matches are not enough to call it a rule.

## Suggested

### Mix targets

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Integrated loudness | -10 to -12 LUFS |
| True peak | under -1 dBFS |
| Voice | always on top; nothing may mask a word |
| Music under voice | 18 to 24 dB below the voice |
| Effects | 6 to 12 dB below the voice, short |

The reel's -9.4 LUFS with clipped peaks is louder than it needs to be. The targets above keep the same punch without distortion.

### Voice

- Cut every breath and every gap longer than about 0.15 s between phrases. The picture edit depends on this tight voice track.
- Light compression and a limiter to hold the level flat.
- Keep one consistent room tone under jump cuts so they do not click.

### Music plan

Follow the measured shape:

| Section | Music |
|---|---|
| Hook | none, or barely there. Let the first words land dry |
| Proof | a low, tense bed starts on the first cut |
| Reveal and stakes | pull the music out. Silence makes the claim feel serious |
| Promise and first steps | light or none |
| Last steps to the end | a driving beat, 120 to 130 BPM, for momentum |
| End CTA | duck or stop the music so the instruction is clear |

Do not cut to the beat. Lay the music under a finished voice edit.

### Effects map

One short sound per kind of event. Keep each under 0.4 s.

| Picture event | Sound |
|---|---|
| Card slides in | soft whoosh |
| Card rises, headline lands | low thud or boom |
| Icon badge pops | soft pop or click |
| Label types on | fast key ticks |
| Highlighter swipe | marker stroke |
| Step coin flips | coin spin and ring |
| Lime label word swap | short tick |
| Kinetic title word | hard hit per word |
| Ball slam and shatter | impact plus debris |
| Negative flash | camera shutter or reversed hit |
| White flash | bright flash or riser tail |
| Punch-in cut | nothing, or a very soft thump |
| Caption beats | nothing. Never add a sound per word |

If the editor has the original reel, ask them to listen for which of these are really there and report back, then update this file.

## Sound checklist

- Can every word be understood on a phone speaker?
- Does the music leave during the most serious claim?
- Is there any effect on a caption pop? Remove it.
- Do the flashes and slams have a sound of their own?
- Does the loudness meter read between -12 and -10 LUFS, with no peak over -1 dBFS?
