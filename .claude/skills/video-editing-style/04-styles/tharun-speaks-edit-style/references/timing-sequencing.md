# Timing and sequencing

Pacing numbers from the reference reel and a section template for new videos. Frames are at 25 fps.

Labels: Measured, Approx, Suggested.

## The three clocks

The edit runs on three clocks at once. Each has its own tempo.

| Clock | What changes | Tempo | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Words | the caption beat | every 0.24 s (median 6 frames) | Measured |
| Pictures | a cut | every 2.0 s (median), 2.35 s (average) | Measured |
| Ideas | a new section | every 3 to 12 s outside the list | Measured |

The viewer's eye always has something new: if the picture holds, the words move; when a sentence ends, the picture changes.

| Rule | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| First cut | 3.44 s | Measured |
| Shortest shot | 0.56 s | Measured |
| Longest shot | 8.48 s, with five card swaps and a row of numerals in its first 4.5 s | Measured |
| A-roll shot, median | 1.70 s | Measured |
| Graphic scene | 1.12 to 3.44 s, median 2.14 s | Measured |
| A-roll share of runtime | 64% | Measured |
| Graphics and inserts share | 36% | Measured |
| Longest run of A-roll with no graphic scene or insert | 9.1 s (61.2 to 70.3 s), split by a negative flash at 64.7 s | Measured |
| Emphasis stacks | about one every 5 s | Measured |
| Stack hold | 12 to 33 frames | Measured |
| Cuts and the music beat | unrelated | Measured |

Working limits for a new edit:

- No more than 2.5 s without a cut, an overlay or a scale change. Suggested, derived from the reel.
- No graphic scene over 3.5 s. Measured.
- No A-roll run over about 9 s without a full-screen scene or insert. Measured.

## Rhythm inside a sentence

A typical sentence in the reel runs like this:

1. Fast single-word beats, 2 to 8 frames each, on a wide framing.
2. A slightly longer beat on the last content word before the turn.
3. A cut, often a punch-in.
4. An emphasis stack on the payoff phrase, built word by word, held 0.5 to 1.3 s.
5. A cut to the next thought or to a graphic.

Fast, fast, fast, hold. The hold is what makes the fast part readable.

## Rhythm inside a list item

Each of the five steps follows one pattern:

| Part | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Announce | 0 to 0.7 s | In two of the five steps the speaker names the step first, touching the matching prop. The other three cut straight to the coin |
| Coin scene | 1.1 to 2.1 s | The step's coin flips up and shows its name |
| Explain | 2 to 5 s | A-roll with badges, or one or two demo cards |
| Button | 0 to 1.2 s | Optional kinetic title or stack that sums it up |

Step lengths in the reel, from the start of one step to the start of the next: 4.6, 6.3, 8.4, 6.4 and 5.6 s. The middle step is the longest and carries the mid-roll CTA.

## Section template

Proportions from the reference reel. Multiply the shares by the target length.

| # | Section | Share | 30 s | 60 s | 90 s | Must contain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 4% | 1.5 s | 2.5 s | 3.5 s | One shot. A physical object. Giant headline behind the head. A one-line promise |
| 2 | Proof | 10% | 3 s | 6 s | 9 s | Who, what, the number, the time. A photo or article card. A number build in captions |
| 3 | Reveal | 3% | 1 s | 2 s | 3 s | One full-screen scene that names the twist |
| 4 | Stakes | 12% | 3.5 s | 7 s | 11 s | "If you are X and not doing Y". A counting headline. Ends on a hero graphic |
| 5 | Promise | 10% | 3 s | 6 s | 9 s | "Here are the N things, in order." Preview cards. Numerals over the props |
| 6 | The list | 37% | 11 s | 22 s | 33 s | N steps, each with a coin scene. Mid-roll CTA after the middle step |
| 7 | Mistake and payoff | 14% | 4 s | 8.5 s | 12.5 s | Why people fail. What following the order gets you. Mostly A-roll |
| 8 | Tool mention and close | 7% | 2 s | 4 s | 6.5 s | One product screen card. One closing claim |
| 9 | End CTA | 3% | 1 s | 2 s | 2.5 s | White card, one instruction, last frame of the video |

For videos under 40 s, drop section 3 and merge sections 7 and 8. Never drop the hook, the list announcements or the end CTA. Suggested.

## Where each device goes

| Device | Position in the video | Count in the reel |
|---|---|---|
| Giant headline behind the subject | hook, proof, stakes: the first 20% | 3 |
| Photo insert | proof | 1 |
| Paper scenes | reveal and stakes | 2 |
| Proof cards behind the head | proof and promise | 6 cards |
| Prop numerals | promise | 1 |
| Step coin | start of every list item | 5 |
| Demo card on white grid | inside the list and at the tool mention | 3 scenes, 4 cards |
| Logo badge behind the shoulder | promise, on the first brand mention | 1 |
| Kinetic title on black | inside the list | 1 |
| Icon badges | inside the list | 1 set of 3 |
| Negative flash | section changes and one punchline | 3 |
| White flash | around bright graphic scenes | 2 |
| CTA card | 59% and 97% of the runtime | 2 |
| Macro insert of a prop | mistake section | 1 |

The first third of the video uses the richest, most varied graphics. The list settles into a repeating pattern. The last quarter is mostly speaker and captions. Spend the motion-graphics budget early.

## Timing a new edit, step by step

1. Lay the cleaned voice on the timeline. Mark every sentence end.
2. Mark sections with the template. Check each share against the table, within a few seconds.
3. Mark keyword phrases. Each gets a stack and usually a cut just before it.
4. Mark every noun that will get a picture. Put the picture's in-point on the word or up to 12 frames before it.
5. Place full-screen scenes on sentence boundaries. Let the voice run under them.
6. Scan for any gap over 2.5 s with no picture change. Add a punch-in, an overlay or a push-in.
7. Put the two flash transitions on the biggest section changes.
8. Watch once with the sound off. The story should still read from captions and graphics alone.
