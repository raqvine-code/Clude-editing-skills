## 3. Style and method
- **Style:** P21 vice-neon titles, learned from your logo-reveal video and two posters.
  - **Fonts:** Pricedown for the game-title words. Noto Serif Display (narrow) for the editorial lines. Patrick Hand for handwritten asides. Inter Tight for labels and numbers.
  - **Colours:** the sunset gradient, indigo through violet, magenta and pink to coral and orange. Lime and hot pink are accents, plus fine grain.
- **Motion-graphics skill:** profile **M2, one-idea concept piece / data poem**, where numbers and counters drive the motion.
  - Example prompts followed: #2 "Photon journey" (giant numbers and a running counter), #4 "What happens in one second" (counters with restraint) and #33 "Talking-head clip edit" (real footage with graphics).
- **The one governing idea:** *GTA V's sales line never fell, so GTA VI's clock kept running.*
  - One visual system carries it: a neon sales line plus a year counter.
  - The video opens on the neon "13" and closes on it.
- **Your rules:** no background music, no colour grading (footage and B-roll keep their own colours), real sound recordings only, no generic stock footage.
