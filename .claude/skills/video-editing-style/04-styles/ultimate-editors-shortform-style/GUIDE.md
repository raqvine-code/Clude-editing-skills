## When to choose this style
| Footage / request | Use |
|---|---|
| Talking-head tips, list or "mistakes" short, clean dark studio shot, premium calm look, editing / design / business topic | **this skill (P15)** |
| Same footage but loud, giant keyword, hidden jump cuts, speaker inside graphics | `event-map-reel-edit-style` P13 |
| Word-pop captions, behind-subject headline, lime labels | `tharun-speaks-edit-style` |
| No camera footage | `faceless-explainer-reel-edit-style` P12 or P14 |
Every short still starts from `shorts-editing-pro` and uses the event-map method; this skill supplies the look.

## 1. Captions (Prompt: two kinds, sans and script; learn placement, colour, animation)
- **M/O** Every caption group = a small sans lead-in + ONE keyword phrase in the script font (a bold italic high-contrast serif). Never two script phrases in one group.
- **M** Keyword colour one accent, `#3D77F3` in the reel, soft same-colour glow on dark footage. Sans white on footage, near-black on off-white.
- **M** Size: keyword 3.7-4.5 % of frame height over the speaker, 6.5 % on full-screen scenes; sans about half of that.
- **M** Placement over the speaker: 49-56 % of the height for the hook block, 62-76 % for section titles, never on the face.
- **O** Animation: word by word on the spoken time; the new word arrives grey and soft then turns white in about 0.1 s; the keyword writes on left to right through a soft blur in about 0.4 s.
- **C** Which words get the script font: the phrase that carries the claim (the mistake, the fix, the number, the last word of the payoff). One per group.
- **C** Fonts on this Mac: sans = the neutral grotesque from `fonts-and-colours`; script = Playfair Display Bold Italic from the central fonts folder until the brief names the real ones. Telugu shorts: keep the same two-tier idea with the Telugu fonts in `fonts-and-colours`.

## 2. Hook (Prompt: hook psychology; keep the speaker on screen; right text combinations between sans and script; show the lines that keep viewers hooked)
- **M** First word on screen at 0.1 s, keyword by 0.4-0.9 s, a UI device in the first second (three numbered pills, one per spoken word), a second device at 3.4 s (slider dragged from Beginner to Professional), first full-screen scene at 8.67 s.
- **M** The speaker stays visible for the whole hook and 69 % of the reel.
- **O** A slow push-in during the first 0.4 s while the lower band darkens.
- **C** The prompt's "between sounds and script" is read as "between sans and script" (dictation slip). The hook line promises a count ("three mistakes") and the step pill makes the count visible, so the viewer waits for 1, 2, 3.

## 3. Animations (Prompt: three kinds of animations, then four categories)
**Three kinds (C, read from the reel; the brief's own names are unknown):**
| Kind | What it is | Reel share (M) |
|---|---|---|
| On-screen | UI or text laid over the speaker, lower band darkened and blurred | 43 % |
| Split | animation on the top 58.5 % of the height, speaker on the bottom 41.5 %, captions on the speaker half | 26 % |
| Full-screen | speaker gone, off-white scene | 31 % |

**Four categories (Prompt), with the reel's example of each (C classification):**
| Category | Reel example |
|---|---|
| Text-centric | "Less is more." alone; "Duller colors always look more high-end." |
| Object-centric | 3D eyeball with two floating UI cards; play-icon card that shrinks 25 %; colour swatches; brand-name cards |
| Character-centric | not in this reel: needs the brief |
| Video-centric | a tilted card playing an example edit while the caption writes beside it |
For each one the prompt says to study the assets, effects, shapes and placement under its description. Those descriptions are in the brief (not received).

## 4. How to create an animation (Prompt order: background, placement, effects on the objects, stylise the scene)
1. **Background (M/O):** off-white `#F0EEEA`, faint square grid, soft diagonal light and shadow bands. Never flat pure white.
2. **Placement (O):** one hero object near the centre, text above or below it, small cards floating around it at different depths, wide empty margins. Elements are small (see `beginner-to-pro-edit-tips` Rule 1).
3. **Effects on objects (O):** soft drop shadows, objects fade and scale in from about 60 %, the camera drifts the whole time. One special effect per reel-section at most (the reel has one particle burst in 50 s).
4. **Stylise (O):** white rounded cards, thin strokes, one accent blue, dull red only for "wrong" things, labels in small caps.

## 5. On-screen animations (Prompt: place the on-screen UIs, darken and blur the background behind them)
- **M** Darken the lower ~45 % of the frame to near black and blur it; keep the face clean; put the UI at 62-76 % of the height.
- **O** UI = small dark rounded pills with thin strokes. Width about 29 % of the frame.
- **O** The 1-2-3 step pill is the spine of a list video: rings for what is coming, white fill for what has been reached, shown again at each number and at the end.
- **C** The prompt's "on-screen eyes" is read as "on-screen UIs" (dictation slip).

## 6. Sound
Follow `ue-sound-design` (three families, four passes). Measured in the reel: a hit on every cut into or out of an animation scene, about 29 hits per minute, bright whoosh-like hits on "Less is more." and on the cut into the "Reserve" scene.

## 8. Do not
- No giant captions, no second accent colour, no pure black / white / red, no effect on every element (the reel's own three rules).
- No scene without the speaker for longer than about 6.6 s (the reel's longest full-screen stretch).
- Do not copy the reel's footage, eyeball, brand cards or example edit. Learn the layout and timing only. No third-party logos in our own edits.
