# Video 1 study: "I Made 8.85 Lakh From Content in One Month. Copy Me"

Studied 2026-11-21. Creator: Aasil Khan (name read from his own subscribe lower third). 1920x1080 (16:9), 29.97 fps. The supplied file is the FIRST 707 s of the video: it stops in the middle of Stage 3, so the ending, outro and end card were not seen.
Topic: an income breakdown ("where every rupee came from") turned into a 3-stage roadmap for creators.
Reference only: never reuse the creator's face, footage, script, screenshots or channel assets.

## How it was studied (coverage)
- Every shot viewed once (227 mid-shot frames on 6 sheets).
- 18 frame-by-frame strips (2-30 fps): hook, recap chart entry and update, light-leak flash, block wipe, framework sequence, split-panel entry, stage intro, staircase pan, typed notes, big-number scene, contrast pair, 3D selector list, right-side caption, timeline overlay, subscribe lower third, then-vs-now montage, self-shot B-roll.

- Face box tracked at 5 fps (2,361 face frames): framing size, punch-in ratio, speaker position.
- Per-frame brightness and colour (21,189 frames): scene classes, flash count.
- Audio: EBU R128 loudness on the original file, stereo side channel (L-R) for music/SFX, level floor per 30 s.
- Word-level transcript (2,492 words) for speech rate, pauses and script order.
- All on-screen text read by OCR at 5 fps (3,535 frames, 2,456 text events), plus a 27-frame sample of caption moments for exact sizes and positions.

## Pacing shape
- Minutes 0-4: 19-25 cuts/min, a new graphic every 2-4 s, flashes every 10-20 s.
- Minute 5 (policy detail): 13 cuts/min, typed white slides of 8-10 s.
- Minutes 6-8: back to 18-24 cuts/min (many short screen-recording shots).
- Minutes 9-11: 10-16 cuts/min; long typed slides (up to 14.3 s) and plain takes of 12-13 s.
So the edit slows as the content gets more detailed: early on it sells, later it teaches from the staircase slide.

## Sound map (Measured)
- Master: -16.5 LUFS, LRA 4.1 LU, true peak -0.3 dBFS. Voice about -19 dBFS RMS. Much quieter and more dynamic than the Tharun long-form master (-10.5 LUFS).
- Music carries the setup, then steps back: the level floor between words is -30 dBFS for the first minute (about 12 dB under the voice), -32/-33 in minute 2, and -36 from 2:00 to the end (a faint bed or none).
- Stereo-only layer: -41 dBFS for 0:00-2:00, -50 to -58 for most of 2:00-9:10 (a little higher, -47, through Stage 1), back up to -45 for 9:10-10:10 as Stage 3 opens. 73% of that layer's energy is below 120 Hz: when music plays it is a bassy bed that stays out of the voice band.
- Sound effects are sparse: a stereo burst lands within 0.3 s of 31% of the flashes, 30% of white-scene entries and 26% of all cuts (chance level 17%). Most cuts are silent; captions are silent.

## Limits
- Only the first 707 s were supplied: no ending, outro or end screen was seen.
- One video from this creator: his constants cannot yet be separated from this video's choices.
- Fonts are matches by eye. Hex colours come from compressed video.
- Sound effects were detected as energy bursts in the stereo layer, not identified by type; mono effects are invisible to that method. The music claim after 2:00 is "faint or absent", not "absent".
- The push-in figure comes from a face box and is noisy.
