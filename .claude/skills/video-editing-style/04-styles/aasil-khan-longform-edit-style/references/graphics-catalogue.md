# Graphics catalogue: every component in V1 ("I Made 8.85 Lakh From Content in One Month")

Studied 2026-11-21. Timings are **Measured** from frame strips (2-30 fps) unless marked (O) = Observed on the one-frame-per-shot sheets. Colours are sampled from full-quality frames (compressed video, so treat as ±5%).
Reference only: rebuild every graphic with your own data and brand. Never reuse the creator's face, footage, screenshots or channel assets.

## 1. The three worlds
| World | Look | Share of runtime |
|---|---|---|
| **A-roll** | Low-key room: speaker in a black tee, waist-up, centred; bookshelf in soft focus; a blue light strip on the right wall, small warm practicals; heavy vignette. Very dark grade (median luma 27/255, highlights ~92/255), cool blue-violet shadows, warm skin | 58% |
| **White "keynote" scenes** | Off-white #FBFBFB-#FDFDFF with a barely visible grid; black bold headings, blue headings, gradient blocks, star bullets, soft drop shadows. Used for data and for the roadmap | 25% |
| **Navy "product UI" scenes** | Pure black with two soft royal-blue glows (#071B6C-#082180) in opposite corners, four tiny white corner dots like artboard handles, faint grid lines; white semibold type with blue #3367FE accents; glass cards with glow. Also frames the screen recordings | about 12% |
| B-roll and memes | Self-shot staged clips with shutter-drag blur and blurred frame edges; one letterboxed meme card | about 5% |

A-roll and graphics alternate by hard cut. A graphic scene rarely runs longer than 7 s in the first half; in the second half typed white slides hold 7-14 s.

## 5. Screen recordings
- Dark pages (video platform, analytics, a services site) are shown as a rounded window on the navy world, never full-bleed; fast scroll steps of 0.3-0.8 s cut together (7:58-8:08: 8 shots in 10 s).
- White pages (marketplace profile, policy text, booking profile, course page) are shown full-bleed like the white scenes, with a slow scroll or push. Key phrases get a highlight box (policy page at 5:36).
- A cursor is visible and does the clicking.

## 6. Transitions
| Transition | How | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | Default everywhere | about 190 |
| **Light-leak flash** | A warm glow enters from one side for 2-3 frames, one frame is almost fully cream-white, the cut sits under it, the new shot starts tinted orange → violet and clears in 6-8 frames (about 10 frames in total). A cool white version is used about a quarter of the time | 26 (2.2 per minute); 20 of them in the first 5 minutes, then one every 30-60 s |
| Two-colour block wipe | A blue bar (#0C70FF) sweeps left to right, a white bar follows it, the white then retreats and reveals the next shot; 21 frames (0.7 s) | 2 (7:23.5 and 11:43.6) |
| Focus pull on entry | Graphic objects start blurred and sharpen in 6-9 frames | several |
| Over-exposed frame | One white-blown frame between two B-roll clips | 1 |
No zoom blurs, spins, glitches or shakes.
