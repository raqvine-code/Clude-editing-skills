**Weak points.** Type cards hide the speaker for 72 % of the time (fine for a showcase, risky for a personal brand);

**Type (O, ≈).** Script: thin, upright-slanted signature script with swash capitals (≈ Great Vibes / Allura / Pinyon Script

- **Quiet ending:** the last line drops to two small boxed subtitle lines, typed on.

**Camera (M).** Two micro-zooms of ≈ 2.5 % over 4 frames (f6-9, f65-68) and two punch-outs of 6-7 % on sentence starts
