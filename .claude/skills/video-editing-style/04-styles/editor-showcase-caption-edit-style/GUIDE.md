# Editor-showcase caption styles (P17-P20), learned 2026-11-27

**Why it exists.** The owner rejected an edit ("I dont like this edit, you need to learnt so much edits") and sent four editor-showcase reels to study: caption styles, typography, text animation, motion graphics, transitions, timing. This skill holds what was measured frame by frame and how to rebuild it with the kit.

**Read with:** `02-craft/text-animation-moves` (stack moves), `02-craft/event-map-reel-edit-style` (event map, sync), `01-process/before-edit/2-PRO-MOVE-MENU.md` (floors), `03-sound-type-assets/pro-font-and-subtitle-guide` (placement research), `04-styles/aesthetic-pro-reel-edit-style` (P16, the client build), `04-styles/ultimate-editors-shortform-style` (P15, the nearest older style to P20).

**Evidence:** `references/reel-notes-R5-R8.md` (every number, with labels), boards in `references/frames/`. Picture measured frame by frame; sound measured from the signal, not listened to; font names are visual guesses.

## 1. What all four share (the grammar)
| Rule | Measured | Refs |
|---|---|---|
| **Captions sit on the chest, under the chin** | block centre at **54-59 % of the height** (53.8 / ≈58 / 58.5); never above the head | R6, R7, R8 |
| **Distance from the chin** | caption centre = chin + **17 % of the height** (range 13-21 %) | R7 |
| **One focal zone** | 96 % of caption frames inside x 30-70 %, y 45-70 %; the eye line holds at one height (26-33 %) through every cut | R7 |
| **Two voices, one hero word** | a tiny light lead-in (1/5 to 1/3 of the hero size) + ONE display word; never two display words fighting | all |
| **Hero word sized to the frame** | sans or caps hero **72-87 % of the width**; a script phrase **50-58 %** | all |
| **One accent colour, taken from the footage or brand** | green + white on B&W (R5), orange on an orange-graded wall (R6), orange on white / lilac (R7), white on indigo (R8) | all |
| **The return to the speaker is a focus pull** | the footage arrives blurred and sharpens in 5-10 frames (0.2-0.35 s) | R5, R6, R7 |
| **The speaker goes into a card** | footage shrinks into a rounded card or phone mock-up in 4-9 frames (0.13-0.3 s); captions keep their screen position | R5, R6, R7 |
| **Small single icons, on the word** | one object 7-12 % of the width (hat, brain, gold bar, glass cube, mask), never a row of emoji | all |
| **Dense sound** | 7.0-9.7 separate stereo sound events per 10 s; -14 to -15 LUFS | all |
| **A wrapper sells it** | Raw / Edit split, price tags, "Edited by" card | all |

## 2. Why it works (the technique, not the look)
1. **The eye never travels.** A viewer reads the face and the words in one fixation when the words sit one hand-width under the chin. R7 says it in its own script ("if you make people's eyes move, you lose their attention") and breaks the rule once, on that exact line, to prove it. Above-head text costs a jump up and back for every phrase.
2. **Hierarchy reads for the viewer.** Read only the hero words of R5: Quality, Wins, Thumbnail, chasing, Follows, slave. The message survives. The small words are there for the ear, the big word is for the thumb-scroller. Choose the hero word by asking "which one word carries the sentence if the sound is off".
3. **Width, not point size.** A long word and a short word both fill the same share of the frame, so the stack edge stays constant and every card looks designed. Size the word until it measures 72-87 % of the width.
4. **Plates, not layers.** R5 and R6 do not put more things on the speaker. They take the speaker away for 0.3-2.5 s (type card, B-roll card, dark card) and bring him back with a focus pull. Each return is an arrival. This is why they feel "edited" while a caption-only edit feels like text on a talking head.
5. **A colour system is a brand.** One hue from the set or the logo, its dark and its light. R6 even regrades the wall to that hue. Nothing on screen falls outside the three colours.
6. **Silence between captions.** R8 leaves the screen empty for 0.26-0.77 s between caption groups. Each group is then an event with an entrance, not a subtitle stream.
7. **The move matches the word.** "slave" rises from below, "chasing" flips colour, follower icons multiply on "follows", the eye-scatter throws words to the corners. A move is chosen by the meaning of its word, never from a list.

## 3. Pick ONE profile
| | P17 type cards + inversion | P18 brand-colour caps + pills | P19 under-chin kinetic | P20 script + small sans |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ref | R5 | R6 | R7 | R8 |

| Lead-in type | light sans / italic, 1/5 size | light sans, tiny | light sans, 1/3 size, dimmed | regular grotesk, 1/4 of the script height |
| Palette | 1 dark colour + white, B&W footage | 1 hue: bright, cream, near-black | white + 1 accent | white only; 1 colour for the tiles |
| Signature moves | colour inversion cut, ghost-to-solid, rise with blur, video into phone | converge from edges, pill wipe, B-roll into UI card | keyword blur-in, drop-blur exit, device card, deliberate scatter | write-on through blur, 1-frame clear, number tiles, glass icon |
| Rhythm | plate change every 0.83 s | a plate lasts ≈ 1.9 s | caption change ≈ every 1 s (by eye) | a caption group every 1.7 s, held 0.9 s |

## 4. Recipes (1080x1920; frames at 30 fps, double them at 60 fps)
| # | Move | Recipe | Kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chest placement** | find the chin y (% of height) in each framing; caption block centre = chin + 17 % (13-21 %). With the chin at 40-45 % this gives 57-62 % (y 1090-1190), inside the house band of 58-65 %. Keep every text block inside x 120-960 | `stacks` `y`, `style.cap_y` |
| 2 | **Two-voice caption** | lead-in small and dim, hero word big; ratio 0.20 (P17), 0.25 (P20), 0.33 (P19); the small line tucks against the hero word's top-left or bottom-right, never centred under it | `stacks` tokens `s` / `b`, `ratio` |
| 3 | **Keyword blur-in** | blurred to sharp in 4-5 frames (0.13-0.17 s); the kit's version is 0.18 s, 35 → 100 % opacity, scale 1.05 → 1 | `stacks` token `fx: "blur"`, or `style.reveal: "blur"` |
| 4 | **Ghost to solid** | the word waits at 30-40 % opacity, goes solid in 5 frames with a 10 px rise | `stacks` `ghost`, `typecard` hero |
| 5 | **Drop-blur exit** | the stack drops 2 % of the height, blurs and fades in 3-4 frames, on the cut | `stacks` `out: "fade"` (0.10 s; the drop is a gap, section 9) |
| 6 | **Write-on through blur** | the script phrase is uncovered left to right behind a soft blurred edge (tool: 20 % of the phrase width), 12-16 frames (0.4-0.53 s) for three words; the small sans words fade in one by one, 3 frames each, alongside; the group clears in ONE frame | `caption_moves.py` `writeon` |
| 7 | **Empty beat between groups** | 0.26-0.77 s with no caption after each group (median 0.44 s); never bridge two phrases with one card | leave gaps between `stacks` / layers |
| 8 | **Focus-pull return** | on every return from a card or B-roll: blur at full on the first frame, sharp in 5-10 frames | `dims` `{"t0": cut-0.3, "t1": cut+0.3, "amt": 1.0, "blur": 16, "fade": 0.3}` (tested: the ramp-up hides under the card) |
| 9 | **Type card** | full-frame plate in the accent colour, hero word 76-87 % wide near mid-frame (≈), tiny lines at its corners; hold 0.3-2.5 s | `caption_moves.py` `typecard` |
| 10 | **Colour inversion cut** | swap plate and type colours for 8-13 frames (0.3-0.5 s) on the stressed word; no transition; layout does not move; up to 4 swaps on one layout | `typecard` `invert` |
| 11 | **Speaker in a card** | the plate closes in round the speaker in 4-9 frames to a rounded card (radius ≈ 5 % of the width); the hero word sits across the card's lower edge | `typecard` `window` (tested) |
| 12 | **Video into a phone** | same, into a phone mock-up; a platform icon pops on it when the platform is named | `typecard` `window` + icon PNG as a second `layers` entry |
| 13 | **Pill captions** | near-black full-radius pill, glow blob in the accent at its left end, small bold caps; wipes in left to right in 6 frames on the word; two pills stacked with a gap of ≈ 1 % of the height for a pair of nouns | `caption_moves.py` `pill` |
| 14 | **Converge from the edges** | two hero words slide in from the left and right edges in 9 frames with motion blur, then a tiny word fades in between them | `caption_moves.py` `converge` |
| 15 | **Rise with motion blur** | the word travels up 10 % of the height in 4 frames, blurred, then sits | `stacks` token `fx: "rise"` |
| 16 | **Number tiles (list device)** | three diamond tiles pop 0.2-0.33 s apart, each ghosted for 2-4 frames then lit; when item 1 is named, tile 1 rolls to sit beside its word (0.55 s, rotation settling) and the others fade | `caption_moves.py` `tiles` |
| 17 | **Icon on the word** | one PNG, 7-12 % of the width, on or beside the hero word; fades in and floats slowly (≈) | `layers` (front) |
| 18 | **The deliberate break** | once per video, on a line about chaos or distraction, throw the words to the frame corners; return to the chest on the next line | `stacks` with `x` / `y` / `align` |
| 19 | **Quiet ending** | the last line drops to small boxed subtitle lines, typed on; no hero word | `captions` plain, or `stacks` `fx: "type"` |
| 20 | **Camera under captions** | micro-zoom 2.5 % in 4 frames on a phrase start; punch-out 6-7 % on a sentence start; 5 framings in 30 s with the eye line fixed | `camera` |

## 5. Build with the kit
```bash
# 1. write a spec (copy tools/example_moves.json), render the moves, check the sheets
python3 tools/caption_moves.py my_moves.json <project>/assets/moves --sheet
# 2. place each move in the EDL (fps in the spec = fps in the EDL)
#    "layers": [{"key": "mg_card1", "dir": "<project>/assets/moves/mg_card1", "t0": 4.60, "t1": 5.90, "behind": false}]   (text-only move: key "txt_...")
#    "dims":   [{"t0": 5.60, "t1": 6.20, "amt": 1.0, "blur": 16, "fade": 0.3}]      <- focus pull on the return at 5.90
# 3. leave no `captions` group under a move; check with `shortkit.py stills`
```
- Front `layers` draw under `stacks` and `captions`, so a stack can sit on a card when needed.
- A `typecard` without `window` hides the speaker: it needs no person matte, so it works where MediaPipe is missing (`PATHS-AND-SETUP.md`, cloud section).
- Tested 2026-11-27: all kinds render (`references/frames/caption_moves_test_board.jpg`), and a whole 47.6 s short was built, rendered, mixed and delivered with them: `references/worked-example/` (PRE-EDIT sheet, PLAN, `build.py` that writes the layer spec + EDL + facts, `qc.py`). Copy `build.py` for the next short.
- Kinds in the tool now: `writeon`, `pill`, `converge`, `typecard` (several heroes, ghost-to-solid `solid_t`, speaker `window` with `release` and a haldi `border` = picture frame, `strike`, `icons`), `tiles` (with `labels`), `quote` (the HL card: brush under each highlighted word as it is spoken, card `pulses` on beats), `mc` (the MC: one pinned word over seven procedural pattern sources). `--only key1,key2` re-renders single moves.
- Kit changes made for it (2026-11-27): shortkit `anchor_track` (the camera follows the speaker's x), B-roll `dy` (shift a clip up so its subject sits inside a card window), the gate no longer counts `txt_` layers or focus-pull `dims`.
- Key the layers: `mg_` (object cards), `hl_` (quote card), `mc_` (match cut), `txt_` (text-only moves).
- Not built yet: letter-level script write-on (the tool wipes the whole phrase), the phone bezel and UI chrome (draw them as a PNG layer), the footage scaling down into the card (the tool crops into it instead), `neg` flash leaves one black frame (kit design).

## 6. What counts for the gate
`01-process/before-edit/2-PRO-MOVE-MENU.md` says text on footage is never motion graphics. For this style:
| Piece | Counts as | EDL key prefix |
|---|---|---|
| Type card with an object (speaker window, phone, icon system, tiles) | designed MG, kind "full scene" or "on-screen UI" | `scene_` / `mg_` |
| Word-only type card | text move (does NOT count) | - |
| Number tiles | designed MG, kind "list device" | `mg_` |
| Device card / middle-box overlay | designed MG, kind "on-screen UI" | `mg_` |
| Pills, write-on, converge, blur-in, inversion on a word-only card | text moves | - |
| Icon on the word | light MG only when it animates with the meaning (multiplying followers, orbiting ring) | `mg_` |
**Key the layers honestly.** The gate counts every `layers` entry as designed MG unless its key starts with `txt_`. Name every text-only move `txt_<name>` (write-on, pill, converge, a word-only type card); keep `mg_` / `scene_` for cards that carry an object. A focus-pull `dims` entry (`amt` 1.0) is not counted either. Gate changed 2026-11-27 to enforce this (it only got stricter).

So P19 and P20 still need the full MG floor from other kinds (B-roll, scenes, HL, MC). P17 and P18 reach it only when at least half the cards carry an object.

## 7. Sound (measured density; kinds inferred, not heard)
- 7.0-9.7 sound events per 10 s: one on every plate change and on most hero words. This matches the house rule "a sound on every insert".
- Map: plate change or card closing in = whoosh; write-on, pill wipe, tile pop, type-on = texture (swipe, tick, pop); inversion cut = short click or camera-shutter; focus-pull return = soft reverse swell into the cut.
- Loudness of the references: -14.1 to -14.9 LUFS. R7 peaks at 0.0 dBTP: do not copy that; master to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.
- Levels and files: `03-sound-type-assets/ue-sound-design`, `03-sound-type-assets/sound-and-music-design`, `02-craft/text-animation-moves` section 5.

## 9. Clashes with house rules (stated, not resolved)
1. **Word timing.** House rule: a word starts on its spoken time, never early (`02-craft/text-animation-moves` rule 3). The references start early so the word is SHARP on the spoken onset: R7 keywords start 6-8 frames before the word; R8 has the script keyword fully on 0.2-0.35 s before it is spoken. Default stays the house rule. To test the reference timing, set a hero token's time 0.15 s before its onset and ask the owner.
2. **Hero width.** These references run hero words to 87 % of the width; the house text zone is x 120-960 (78 %). Keep 78 % for client work.
3. **Above-head text.** None of the four uses it. The house budget of 1-3 above-head accents stays an upper limit, not a target.
4. **Exit drop.** `stacks` has no drop-and-blur exit; `out: "fade"` is the nearest.

## 10. Event grid for a 30-45 s short in this style
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0-0.4 s | focus pull on frame 0 + the hook's hero word arriving (converge, highlight box or write-on); a designed object by 1.0 s |
| every ≈ 1 s | a caption change inside the focal zone |
| every 2-2.5 s | framing change (punch 6-7 %, cut) with the eye line fixed |
| every 6-8 s | a plate: object type card, speaker window, B-roll card or tiles; return with a focus pull |
| once | the deliberate break (recipe 18) on the line that earns it |
| turn (≈ 2/3) | the strongest card: inversion or the video-into-phone scene |
| last 2 s | quiet ending (recipe 19) or the hook's look again for the loop; never end on black or mid-word (R6 and R8 both do) |

## 11. QC for this style (add to the normal QC)
- Caption block centre measured on 5 stills: 54-62 % of the height, and chin + 13-21 %.
- Read only the hero words in order: does the message survive?
- Count hero words on screen at once: never more than one.
- Colours on screen: accent, its dark, its light, white. Anything else is a mistake.
- Every return to the speaker has the focus pull; every plate change has a sound.
- Spell-check every card against the audio (R6 shipped "TEN TIME").
- Thin script at phone size: view a still at 25 % zoom; if the script is not readable, raise its weight or opacity.

## 12. Limits
- Four reels, one viewing pass each, picture only for the detail. Counts from scripts are exact for those files; "≈" values were read off sheets.
- The four are editors' adverts: 11-32 s, built to show range. A 45-60 s client short needs the same grammar at a lower density (P19 base).
- Reference videos are other creators' finished work: study only; never reuse their footage, faces or branding.
