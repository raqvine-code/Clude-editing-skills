# Short-form (9:16) styles learned

Pick ONE style per short. Source skill in brackets. Numbers are at 30 fps unless stated.

## 1. Tharun Speaks reel [`tharun-speaks-edit-style`] - Measured from 2 reels (25 fps)
- Captions: Inter Tight Bold ~116 px, white, no box/stroke, two-layer soft shadow; centred at y 1304 (68 %) (reel B 55 %); hard pop, 1 word per beat, beat median 0.24 s; stacks = small lead-in 0.72x + keyword 1.30-1.75x + trailer.
- Behind-subject headlines: Bebas Neue/Anton, all caps, top third, popped word by word 3-10 frames apart; captions hidden under them.
- Camera: punch-ins 124-143 % on jump cuts, return 0.75-0.79x; hook push 100->116 % over 1.7 s; slow push 1.2-3.6 %/s on long takes.
- Cuts follow the voice (not the beat); median shot 2.0-2.4 s; A-roll 48-64 %; no A-roll run over ~9 s without a graphic.
- Graphics appear on their word or up to 12 frames before; almost nothing animates out. Proof cards, icon badges, photo inserts with B&W + colour sweep, step coins, lime label cards, QR CTA.
- Transitions: hard cut (30/35), negative flash (1 inverted + 1 black frame, 2-4 per video), white flash, blur-zoom. No dissolves/wipes/glitch/shake.
- One accent family per video (lime #D6FD00 or neon green #D4FD2D on navy). Loudness measured ~ -9.5 LUFS.

## 2. Deepak reel method [`reel-editing-method`, `my-editing-rules` s6] - delivered videos
- From a long video: 5 strongest moments as 40-60 s shorts, rough cuts first, then polish (colour + captions first).
- Elements enter 0.05-0.1 s before their word; captions 0.6-1.5 s per phrase; keyword slams 0.4-0.6 s; cards 2-10 s.
- Open on the thesis line at frame 0, end on the payoff word (loops); pauses trimmed to <= 0.3 s.
- Cut music once, on the most important line; no speed ramps on talking heads; rhythm big -> small -> big.

## 3. P1 Glass Spotlight motion ad [`pro-video-editing`] - Measured
Cut-out subject in spotlight, glass phone frame behind, 3-tier type (light lead / lime script accent / heavy keyword + lime period), sparkles, comet streaks, blur-in 3 f, keyword slam 4 f with motion blur, whip transitions; music-led 7-8 s.

## 4. P2 Editorial Red studio [`pro-video-editing`] - Measured
Light seamless studio, giant red italic words built letter by letter (~3 f/letter, completing on the spoken word), red pill tags on photo cards, energy streak + halftone ring transitions, pixel squares.

## 5. P3 Podcast Magazine [`pro-video-editing`] - Measured
Podcast clip laid out as a maroon/cream magazine page with top category labels, serif italic emotional keyword + small sans lead, B-roll strips with red eye-bars, white bar wipe reveals (3 f), highlight boxes.

## 6. P4 Before/After showcase shell [`pro-video-editing`] - Measured
Static shell: small greyscale raw card + big edit card with price/label pills, CTA `Comment "WORD"`; or glossy results card with stat graph and neon pill captions.

## 7. P5 Red-gradient explainer Short [`pro-video-editing`] - Measured
16:9 content letterboxed with "watch full video" CTA; frame shrink-to-card (~1.3 s), glass UI cards, giant behind-head words then 1.1x punch-in, side panel bullets, underline-progress captions, hexagon transition.

## 8. Hormozi talking-head [`top-editor-styles`] - Published
Cuts every 1-3 s, no pauses, medium/close alternation, all-caps heavy captions word by word with yellow/green highlight, emoji/pop-ups; widely copied, so change one element to own it.

## 9. Iman Gadzhi shorts captions [`top-editor-styles`] - Published
Lowercase clean sans, each word shifts light -> bold as spoken; one palette; smooth zooms.

## 10. Motivational [`motivational-video-editing`] - delivered
Story-led colour/type/pacing from 3 references; avoid "only text and basic sound effects" (v1 rejection).

## 11. Dense intro / cold open [`video-intro-design`] - delivered
Pixel-exact punch-ins, giant type behind the head (person matte), dither-through-black, phone-speaker-safe SFX.

## 12. Faceless explainer reel (P12 editorial cutout explainer) [`faceless-explainer-reel-edit-style`] - Measured from the "zip 1" analysis pack (2026-11-21)
No footage: cutout subjects on an off-white studio wall for a rule-of-three hook, one light-leak flash at 37% of runtime, then dark glow scenes built around one object each (pie, statue, traffic light). Blur-in two-font captions (bold sans + italic serif, 6-8 f per word), logo stat wedge (percent x 3.6°), object-with-a-state for contrast pairs, one caption-free line, a sound on every scene change, one sub boom, no music. 10 scenes in 29.9 s. Profiles P6-P11 are listed in `04-styles/pro-video-editing/GUIDE.md`.

## 13. Two-tier kinetic talking head (P13) [`event-map-reel-edit-style`] - Measured from the "zip 2" analysis pack (2026-11-22)
Talking head at 204-224 wpm where the edit carries the pace. Small sentence-case line + ONE big keyword per group on the chest (3-9 words, about 1.3 s, clears in 1 f); keyword colour = role (gold topic, red threat, white + orange bar pivot). Three modes switched about every 2.5 s in the breaths: speaker + captions, speaker composited into graphics, full-screen metaphor cutaway. Jump cuts hidden by push-through moves (12 f out, 16 f in), graphics or a new framing of 15 % or more. Hook push-in x1.6 by f55; lists as pedestals or zoom-bumps; luma flip or light-leak at the turn (60-77 % of runtime); about 9 SFX per 10 s.

## 14. Faceless 2.5D documentary story (P14) [`event-map-reel-edit-style`] - Measured from the "zip 2" analysis pack (2026-11-22)
Voice-over story (history, news, mystery) at 130-151 wpm, no camera footage, no music. Stills built as layered dioramas, camera never stops (rotate-and-pull-back on every plate, 1.3-2.5 s). Scene changes are moves (window zoom-through, irises, spin-portal, texture bridge, card push-up), only 2-10 hard cuts in about 41 s. No captions: text only when the word is the picture (3D verdict caps with one red word, typed date card, one-word serif moral). Red = danger, green = life, B&W = death / past; halftone screen as glue. Sound = tick (small event), whoosh-boom (new place), sub boom (meaning word).

Method for every short (same skill): event map in the plan, something new at least every 1.3 s, every visual on a word, every change with a sound.

## 15. Ultimate Editors sans + script talking head (P15) [`ultimate-editors-shortform-style`] - Master prompt + one measured reel (2026-11-22)
Calm premium tips / list short at 191 wpm, speaker on screen 69 % of the time. Captions = small sans lead-in + ONE keyword phrase in a bold italic serif, accent blue `#3D77F3`, keyword only 3.7-4.5 % of frame height. Three layouts: UI pills over a darkened + blurred lower band (43 %), split with animation on the top 58.5 % (26 %), full-screen off-white `#F0EEEA` scenes with one hero object (31 %). A 1-2-3 step pill tracks the numbered points. One special effect in 50 s. Sound: `ue-sound-design`. The brief the master prompt links to has not been received; three kinds / category examples are Claude's reading of the reel.

## Platform rules for every short [`pro-video-editing/library/shortform-online-research.md`]
Hook in 1.5-3 s (visual interrupt + specific promise), 15-30 s sweet spot unless story needs 40-60 s, burned-in captions, keep text/faces out of top ~210 px, bottom ~340-480 px, right ~100 px; master -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP (your rule; references are louder).
