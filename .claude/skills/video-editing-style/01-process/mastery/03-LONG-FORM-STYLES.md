## Shared long-form rules (all skills agree)
1. Hook pays its promise immediately; no logo/intro.
2. Something new every 5-15 s (Deepak: one meaningful visual change ~every 5 s, landing 2-3 s earlier than feels safe).
3. Keep the face on screen for human/emotional beats; full-screen graphics for evidence.
4. Every number/name on screen comes from the script with a source.
5. Transcript-first workflow, stills QA before the long render, -14 LUFS master.
