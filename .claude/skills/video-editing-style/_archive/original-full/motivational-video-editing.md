# What I learnt from the 3 motivation videos
Ref A = Tharun Speaks (6 habits, maroon). Ref B = maroon-sweater creator (learn skills faster, notebook world). Ref C = podcast Odyssey video (cream + red). Colours are named by eye (not measured hex). Principles only, never exact parts. Deepak's rules in `mistakes-to-avoid` always win.

## Colour I learnt
- **Pick one world colour + one accent.** A: maroon + black, white text. C: cream + black + one red. B: black/navy + paper, red and navy ink.
- **A second colour is only a code**, never decoration: B's four sticky squares (grey, yellow, blue, red) = steps 1-4; A's green/yellow/red only inside gauges (good -> bad).
- **Red key word on white text** is the cheapest way to stress a word (C).
- Next video: choose the accent with Deepak; not violet, not default maroon.

## Font / text style I learnt
- **Big hero word:** ultra-condensed heavy white caps, placed behind his head, with a tiny line above or below it (A, B). Use Impact / Archivo narrow from his list.
- **Two voices:** a bold sans for punch + a serif italic or thin sans for the human line (C). Use Tactic Sans / Inter + EB Garamond Italic.
- **Keyword caption:** 1-3 bold words; two-line stack = small word over huge word (B).
- **Hand-lettered words and circles** only inside paper/notebook scenes (B).

## Text animation I learnt
- Words arrive **one by one** on black, pause 1-2 s, the key word turns the accent colour (C question cards).
- A list **types onto a notepad** one item at a time (C); icon chips blur in per line.
- Hero word **pops up from the bottom / punches in** on the stressed word, then leaves with the sentence (A, C).
- Active step **grows and glows, others fade** (B).
- Numbers **count up** (flip-clock / counter) and bars **rise with ease-out** (A, B).

## Pacing I learnt
- About **one cut every 2.5 s**; a quarter of the cuts are under 1 s apart (montages), the rest 3-6 s.
- **Montage of 0.5 s shots** (hero words, photos, "I did it" proof) at the emotional turn, then a long calm hold.
- Film/emotional footage holds 3-6 s; a question on black holds 2.5 s.
- Nothing static longer than ~5 s while he talks.

## Story I learnt
1. Hook in 10 s: a hard question or a belief he breaks.
2. Promise the structure ("6 things", "4 steps", "3 lessons").
3. Each point = claim -> one personal story or example -> one-line takeaway (this line gets the hero word).
4. A breather (tool/sponsor plug, black card) every 2-3 points.
5. Recap card of all points, then ONE action (write one goal / answer one question).
6. End on a held black card with one line.

## Visual devices I learnt
- **One returning object** carries the video (water tank, 4 coloured squares, lotus flower). Pick one.
- **Numbered chapter cards** with a counter, recap before the end.
- **Paper / notebook scene** for steps (hand, markers, circles), PiP of him in the corner.
- **Torn paper reveals a chart**; floating tiles stack on black; frosted tags beside his head.
- **Sponsor plug** keeps the video's look: logo morph, counting stats, "link in description" chip.

## Transitions I learnt
Hard cut between talking head and full-screen scene; short B&W or inverted-colour flash; shrink-to-B&W pause beat; zoom punch-in on the hero word; whip / hand-flip into the paper scene. Slow fades only on film footage.

## SFX I learnt (inferred from structure, I cannot hear)
Hit on each hero word, whoosh on scene swaps and paper flips, soft ticks as list items type in, riser before a chapter card, silence or music drop on the last black card. Levels: `sound-and-music-design`.

## B-roll I learnt
- **B-roll always carries words:** never bare. Word-by-word lowercase caption on top, footage dimmed so text reads (A, B, C).
- **Dim it, then caption it:** archival or class footage darkened, one stacked caption (small word over big word).
- **Match the idea, not the word:** night city for "ambition", doomscroll/party clips for distraction, class footage for "school" (B, C).
- **Short holds:** 2-4 s normal, 0.5 s in a montage; epic film footage (fire, rowing) 3-6 s as emotional punctuation (C).
- **Screen recordings** for tool/software points, with caption words (B).
- **Archival clips** (a famous speaker, news clip) as proof, with an app screenshot cutaway (B).
- **Same grade/colour mood** across clips: dimmed, slightly desaturated, so it sits inside the video's palette.
- **Letterboxed film footage** with slow fades is a separate "cinema" mode, used only for story beats (C).
- **Cut back to him** on the takeaway line; B-roll never covers the hero word.
- For Deepak: pick from `broll-library` first, 1440p+ only, use `png-library` pop-ups for logos/products.

## Do NOT copy (breaks Deepak's rules)
- Colour world behind his cut-out (A1) -> hero words go on his real wall; full colour worlds only when he is off screen.
- Arrows across footage (B4) -> only inside full-screen paper scenes.
- Sentence captions (E11) -> 1-4 words.
- Tilted cards (D10), text under 24 px (C7).
- Theory diagrams for the AI concepts -> show each concept as a real thing (product screen, example, number, story).

## Plan checklist for "motivation + 5 AI concepts"
Hook + promise -> choose ONE returning object -> 5 concept chapters (claim, example, takeaway + hero word) -> breather at the middle -> recap card of the 5 -> one-action CTA on black -> check against `mistakes-to-avoid` -> 2-3 style frames + sync clip before the full render.

## What made the v2 redo work *(video 13, 2026-10-13: he rejected v1 as "only text and basic sound effects")*
- **A built visual answers each spoken idea**, about every 5 s: UI doing the task, a funnel, keycaps, a decision gate, orbs, the 5 lanes, the B&W pause. A caption is never the answer on its own.
- **Real footage:** 19 Pexels B-roll slots, all 2560×1440 renditions, dimmed and captioned. Indian people where the line is about Indian students, teachers or founders. In-points are picked on the word (the bulb lights on "idea").
- **Proof beats:** a real news headline highlighted, and a match cut of five real sources on the role he names.
- **Two reference systems kept apart:** crimson glass for lists and product, charcoal chalk for ideas.

## Applied on video 13 "Top 5 Skills to Stand Out in the AI Era" (2026-10-13, awaiting his review)
- **Returning object = the 2026 year grid** (365 cells, 75 left, echoing the "75 Days TO GO" monitor on his set): hook (Jan 1), "75 days left" count-down 365 → 75, "one AI tool a day", "→ 2027", and the close where the last 75 cells fill one by one ("DAY 75 / EVERY DAY").
- **Hero words printed on his real wall** (multiply ink, person matte in front, x1.08 punch-in): about 30 in 12 min, beside his head where Vision head mattes sampled every 2 s show free wall; long words fall back to a keyword caption.
- **Words arriving on black** for his questions ("What's Gemini's latest model?", "On a stage, in English, before 100 people?", the closing question), then ONE held black card "See you in 75 days." with silence.
- **Many outputs → one choice** = a prompt bar + dots counting 100 → 1,000 + one red dot / "1 / TASTE" (the 100-fake-thumbnail version was rejected, see `mistakes-to-avoid` 10b).
- Full-screen scenes hard-cut in and out while he keeps talking (bars, task window, six fixes, chat, office vs hospital, learning curve); about one change every 4–6 s, with no gap over ~6 s left without a beat.
