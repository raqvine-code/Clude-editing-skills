- **Smooth short-form pacing (ref61, Deepak's benchmark for a long → short clip, measured):**
  - **Story:** one complete thought, thesis → example → consequence → turn → payoff. Open on the thesis line at frame 0; end on the payoff word with no outro.
  - **Dialogue:** 3.9 words/s, pauses trimmed to ≤0.3 s, comma breaths kept.
  - **Picture:** cuts every ~1.1 s (0.25–2.4 s), each landing on a word boundary (median 0.02 s before the next word), on phrase starts or key nouns, while the voice runs unbroken. Each new shot carries the next idea.
  - **Captions:** word by word, with one big accent-colour keyword per phrase over small connector words. The group resets on each cut and sits in the shot's empty space.
  - **Grade:** one consistent look, with one accent colour matching the captions.
  - Full notes: `experiment/11_reference_59_longform_to_shorts_reframe.md`. — *Deepak, 2026-10-06: "smooth paced editing, learn from these"* — `(2026-10-06)`
