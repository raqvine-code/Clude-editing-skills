# Device library: graphics, text and annotation (measured recipes)

The rule behind every device: **the VO names it, the screen shows it, on the word**. One literal picture per idea,
animated so its *state* carries the meaning (a wedge fills, a lamp turns green, a line falls). Frames at 30 fps.

## Data and evidence

**Stat wedge** (R0) — light-grey disc (≈50 % W) behind the subject's head with the brand logo ghosted grey; a wedge
radial-wipes clockwise from 12 o'clock to exactly stat % × 360° over ≈34 f; inside the wedge the logo shows in brand
colour.

**Graph pair** (R2) — a red rising line draws over the exact clause (72 f for "from childbirth fever at alarming
rates") while the camera chases the pen tip (≈290 px/s at 720 W, rotating ≈−11°). Later, the inverse: a green
falling stepped line whose area fill floods in 1 f on "dropped", with a 1 s mono sub drop at ≈−15 dBFS. Put real
numbers on the axes and make the curve match the claim.

**Highlighter → circle → punch** (R3) — highlighter runs line by line left to right at 18–20 f per line (yellow, or
lime `#60ED28`); then a hand-drawn red ellipse (`#DB351F`, 4 px, ≈8 f) around the number ≈10 f after the VO says it;
then a ×1.35 punch-in 9–12 f later on the next clause. Teaches the eye where to look, then rewards it.

**Live-to-print** (R3) — see `transitions.md`: the person becomes a headline (zoom out ×0.45, desaturate 7 f).

**Typed date card** (R3) — condensed caps, white on `#17181B`, ≈45 % W; 1.9 f per character with a bar cursor
blinking on a ≈12 f cycle; a red trim-path underline (5–6 px) trails the text by 8 f; the block drifts up ≈300 px
with ease-out; start typing 10 f before the spoken date. Make it ≥ 5 % H and the underline brighter than R3's
`#921419` on near-black.

**Name pill** (R3) — red `#E61C1F` pill, 46 % W × 5 % H, white bold sans, on the subject's chest; expands from a
small block in 8 f with motion blur; reappears "printed" in the next shot. A name on screen that the VO never says
adds a fact without slowing the voice.

## Lists

**Pedestals** (R4) — one pedestal per item rising in 12–16 f; the item's icon drops on its keyword (±4 f; clock icon
at f492 for the word at f488) with a boom + hit; the label is printed on the pedestal instead of a caption.

**Zoom-bumps** (R1) — see SKILL.md section 8: +7 to +15 % per item, 6–8 f, 1–4 f before the label.

**Chapter cards** (R1) — see `transitions.md`.

## Identity and metaphor

**Identity sandwich** (R4) — rotoscoped speaker / stock crowd / giant title (cap ≈19 % W, bleeding off both edges,
behind the heads) / brand-colour backdrop, revealed on the exact identity word ("realtor").

**Object behind the head** (R4) — a 3D topic object rises from behind the rotoscoped speaker over ≈20 f with rim
light, under a slow 1.0 → 1.19× push over 48 f that ends in a transition.

**Metaphor cutaway** (R4, R0) — one literal object per idea (brain in robot hands for AI, pie for share, traffic
light for stop/start, statue for wealth, bills for money, chess for strategy), ≤ 2.6 s, with a background colour
drift across the sentence (neutral → red for a threat). Captions move to the top 12–21 % band while the object owns
the centre.

**Dim-and-overlay idiom** (R4) — speaker to ≈46 % brightness and defocused over 30 f; a literal pun of the idiom on
top ("upper hand" = a giant hand) without typing the idiom.

**Mascot pun** (R1) — on the payoff adjective ("robotic") a 3D character flies up from below with rotation over
≈12 f, stereo pop on entrance (±20 ms); the black text block re-centres as words are added.

**Brain in a cage / video card** (R1) — the white-canvas cutaway: the speaker's live video in a rounded card, a
literal metaphor object (caged brain for "learn word by word"), small decorative copy as texture. Use on-topic
copy: R1's filler paragraph described computer scripts.

## Text devices

**Two-tier caption group** (R1, R4) — format B, section 3.

**Hook search bar** (R4) — the spoken question types into a search field at speech pace (≈22 chars/s) inside a dark
app UI the speaker has shrunk into (1.0 → 0.35× in 14 f).

**Single-word moral** (R2) — on the thesis sentence only: one bold italic serif word at a time at 34–39 % H; swap
0–4 f after each onset with a 7–11 kHz tick 1–2 f before; spotlight cone, a hand rising into it, −23 %/s pull-back,
6 f of defocus on the last word before the cut; preceded by a hard luma flip (≈31 → ≈203).

**One-word serif for abstract lines** (R3) — italic serif, white with a soft shadow, centred, 20–36 % W; first word
pops 1.42 → 1.0 in 6 f, the rest hard-cut; lead the VO by ≈2 f. For words with no picture ("possible infection").

**Twist word stack** (R3) — black void (`#161F22`); words added upward on a 3D column tilted so lower words grow
and blur; camera rises ≈500 px/s at 1080 W; exit by card push-up. Removing all imagery is the pattern break.

**Verdict caps** (R2, R3) — ultra-black extruded 3D caps, one red word per block, cap ≈7.8 % W; also the CTA voice.

**Dual-font word reveal** (R0) — format A, section 5.
